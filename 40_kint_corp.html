Our History

From the Railroad to
the Restaurant

The Kint story is one of the more unusual in Pennsylvania business — a company that started supplying CO2 fire extinguishers to railroads and ended up becoming an indispensable partner to the restaurant and hospitality industry. That kind of reinvention takes vision. The fact that they kept both service lines running and growing takes discipline. Brian Kint carries that legacy forward today.

Founded

George A. Kint Starts with the Railroads

George A. Kint Inc. is founded supplying CO2 fire extinguishers to the railroad industry in Central Pennsylvania — a demanding, precision-driven customer that set the standard for everything that followed.

Expand

From Railroads to Soda Fountains

Kint begins supplying CO2 to soda fountains in downtown Harrisburg — a pivot that planted the seed for what is now the company's beverage gas division. The same gas, a very different customer, and a big idea about what the business could become.

Growth

Building Expertise Across Two Industries

As the beverage industry booms and fire protection regulations grow more demanding, Kint grows with both. Installation, inspection, maintenance, and delivery capabilities are added — one service at a time, always with the same commitment to reliability.

Today

Brian Kint Carries the Legacy Forward

Brian Kint leads Kint Corp as President, operating out of Harrisburg and Aston, PA — delivering CO2 to bars and breweries, keeping commercial kitchens fire-safe, and honoring a name that Central Pennsylvania businesses have trusted for generations.
