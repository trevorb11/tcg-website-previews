Our History

70+ Years of Fire Protection Excellence

Interstate Fire & Safety was founded in 1951 by Joseph Bucci as a one-person fire extinguisher company serving the local community. Over the following seven decades, through the leadership of William Barnes Jr. beginning in 1971, the company grew steadily into a full-service fire protection organization serving multiple states across the Northeast.

Today, Interstate Fire & Safety serves clients across Connecticut, New Jersey, Massachusetts, Rhode Island, Nassau and Suffolk Counties in New York, and New York City — with a fully trained staff and a comprehensive range of fire protection services built on the same foundation of outstanding customer service that has defined the company since day one.

Compliance & Code

Keeping You Code-Compliant

Fire protection codes and regulations vary by state, municipality, and occupancy type — and they change regularly. Our team maintains current knowledge of all applicable codes and standards across every jurisdiction we serve.

We work with property managers, building owners, and facility managers to ensure their fire protection systems meet or exceed all current requirements — and to document that compliance thoroughly for insurance and regulatory purposes.

  • NFPA 72 — National Fire Alarm and Signaling Code
  • NFPA 13 — Standard for the Installation of Sprinkler Systems
  • NFPA 10 — Standard for Portable Fire Extinguishers
  • NFPA 17A — Standard for Wet Chemical Extinguishing Systems
  • State and local fire codes across CT, NJ, MA, RI, and NY
  • Insurance carrier compliance documentation and reporting
