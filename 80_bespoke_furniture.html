Our Process

From Your Vision to Your Space

Every project follows a process that begins with deep listening and ends with white-glove delivery of something made specifically for you.

Step 01

Consultation

We learn about your space, your vision, your lifestyle, and your specifications. Dimensions, materials, finishes, and function are all discussed before a single line is drawn.

Step 02

Design Development

Detailed drawings, material samples, and finish options presented for your review. We refine until every detail is exactly right before we move to production.

Step 03

Craftsmanship

Your piece is built by our Los Angeles artisan team with the attention it deserves. We provide production updates and welcome you to visit the workshop during the build.

Step 04

Delivery & Installation

White-glove delivery and professional installation. We don't leave until every piece is placed exactly where it belongs and you're fully satisfied with the result.

About Doron Silverman

A Designer and Maker
at Heart

Doron Silverman founded Bespoke Furniture Inc with a conviction that most furniture -- even expensive furniture -- falls short of what it could be. When something is truly designed for a specific person and a specific space, it becomes something more than furniture. It becomes part of the story of that space.

Doron's background extends across the luxury goods world -- from fashion to fine furniture -- and that breadth of experience in bespoke craftsmanship informs everything about how Bespoke Furniture operates. He has collaborated with artists including Jon Buscemi, with whom he exhibited work at the Double Extra Gallery in downtown Los Angeles, bridging the worlds of furniture design and fine art.

The team Doron has assembled reflects his values. The artisans in the Los Angeles workshop have been with Bespoke Furniture for an average of nearly fifteen years. That tenure is not an accident -- it reflects a culture of pride in the work, fair treatment of the people who do it, and the belief that the best furniture is made by people who genuinely care about what they're building.

Doron Silverman
