Our Story

Second Generation. Same Standards.

Todd King's father started this shop in Erie, PA in 1990. Todd grew up around the machines, learned the trade, and eventually took over a company that had already built its reputation the hard way — one precision part at a time. Erie is a manufacturing town and King Precision is exactly the kind of shop Erie produces: no-nonsense, skilled, and reliable.

When Todd took over, he didn't reinvent the shop — he formalized it. ISO 9001 certification brought structure to the quality processes that were already there. ITAR compliance opened the door to defense customers who needed a domestic machining partner they could trust. More than 35 years in, the shop holds the same tolerances and ships on the same timelines it always has.
