About GloTech

Engineering Excellence Since 1996

GloTech Corporation was founded in 1996 by Dr. Wang Zhao with a single purpose: deliver accurate, cost-effective, and reliable flow monitoring technology for the water and wastewater industry. In nearly three decades, that purpose has never wavered. We have simply gotten better at it.

Headquartered in Rockville, Maryland, GloTech provides rapid-turnaround, turnkey product development and manufacturing. We design and build components used in water and wastewater applications around the world, combining deep engineering expertise with efficient manufacturing that keeps lead times short and quality high.

Rongying Cui serves as Founder and CFO, overseeing strategic growth and ensuring GloTech continues to invest in the technologies that matter most to our customers and to the health of the water systems communities depend on. Call us at (516) 225-8348 to talk through your project requirements.

GEM2 Series

Electromagnetic Flow Meters

The GEM2 Series is GloTech's flagship electromagnetic flow meter line and a direct result of nearly 30 years of focused engineering in this specific application. Built for accuracy across variable flow rates and demanding installation conditions, the GEM2 is trusted by utilities and equipment providers who cannot afford measurement errors.

  • Superior measurement accuracy across variable flow rates
  • Flexible installation options for diverse site conditions
  • Premium materials and workmanship for long service life
  • Suitable for water, wastewater, and industrial process applications
  • Compatible with multiple industries and pipe configurations
