Our Story

Founded on the Belief That Everyone Deserves Care at Home

Brian Carrigan founded Remain At Home Healthcare after spending two decades in leadership roles across the home health and hospice industry. He saw firsthand how many people — particularly seniors and workers affected by occupational illness — struggled to navigate complex systems to access the care they were owed.

RAHHC was built to change that. We're based in Athens, Georgia, and we specialize in bringing high-quality, personalized care directly to our clients' homes — while also guiding eligible individuals through the process of accessing no-cost benefits they may not even know they have.

Every care plan we create is personal. Every relationship we build is built on respect. We don't believe in one-size-fits-all solutions, because the people we serve have earned something better than that.

EEOICP Benefits

Nuclear Workers May Qualify for No-Cost Home Care

The Energy Employees Occupational Illness Compensation Program (EEOICP) provides benefits to current and former employees of the Department of Energy and its contractors who developed certain illnesses as a result of their work.

Many eligible workers don't know they qualify — or find the enrollment process too complex to navigate alone. That's exactly what we're here for. Our team has deep expertise in the EEOICP program and guides families through every step of the process.

Check Your Eligibility

Who Qualifies?

Former DOE employees and contractors who developed work-related illnesses including cancers and beryllium disease.

What's Covered?

No-cost in-home healthcare, medical screenings, travel reimbursements, and home modifications.

How We Help

We guide you through eligibility determination, enrollment paperwork, and ongoing benefit management.

No Cost to You

Our assistance navigating EEOICP benefits is provided at no out-of-pocket expense to eligible clients.
