Who We Are

Built for the Serious. Open to Everyone.

Absolute Recomp was founded in 2015 with a clear vision: build the gym that serious athletes actually want to train in, then open the doors to everyone who's ready to bring that same mindset to their fitness.

We're not a big-box warehouse with 400 treadmills. We're a pro-style facility with premium equipment, expert training staff, and an environment built to produce results. Whether you're a competitive bodybuilder, a weekend warrior, or someone who's finally decided to make a real change — you belong here.

With locations across the Dallas-Fort Worth area and more opening in Chicago, Arlington, and Phoenix, Absolute Recomp is building a national presence for a community that takes training as seriously as we do.

All of our clubs are open and staffed 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. Because real athletes don't train on the gym's schedule — they train on theirs.
