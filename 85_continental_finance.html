About Continental Finance

20 Years of Financial Inclusion

Continental Finance Company was founded in 2005 with a mission rooted in financial inclusion: ensuring that Americans with less-than-perfect credit have access to the credit tools they need to participate fully in the financial system.

Based in Newark, Delaware, we are one of America's leading marketers and servicers of credit cards issued by partner banks for underserved consumers. Over the past two decades, we have helped millions of people take meaningful steps toward better financial health — not by lowering standards, but by providing a structured, transparent path to responsible credit use.

Our "low and grow" philosophy means we meet customers where they are, give them a starting point, and support them as they build the kind of credit history that opens doors.

We believe access to credit is a foundation for financial opportunity — and we are committed to making that access fair, transparent, and genuinely empowering for every customer we serve.
