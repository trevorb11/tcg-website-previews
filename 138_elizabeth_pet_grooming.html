What We Offer

Complete Grooming Services
for Your Beloved Pet

From a refreshing bath to a full style session, we offer everything your pup needs to look and feel their absolute best.

🛁

Bathing & Drying

A thorough, soothing bath using premium, pet-safe shampoos and conditioners. We leave your pet's coat clean, fresh, and wonderfully soft.

✂️

Haircuts & Full Grooming

Breed-specific styling and full grooming packages that keep your pet looking sharp, neat, and breed-appropriate all year round.

👂

Ear Cleaning

Gentle, careful ear cleaning that removes dirt and debris, helping to prevent infections and keep your pet comfortable and healthy.

💅

Nail Trimming

Safe, precise nail trimming to keep your pet's paws comfortable and prevent overgrowth. We're gentle with even the most nail-shy pups.

🐾

Paw Treatments

Moisturizing paw pad treatments that soothe dry, cracked paws and protect against the elements — because soft paws are happy paws.

🦷

Teeth Cleaning

Professional dental brushing to freshen breath and support your pet's overall oral health, using pet-safe toothpaste and techniques.
