What We Do

Plain and Simple — We Buy Your Future Payments

If you received a legal settlement that pays you in installments over time, you own a structured settlement. Those payments have real value today — and you have the legal right to sell some or all of them in exchange for a lump sum of cash.

Frequently Asked Questions

Questions We Hear Most Often

What exactly is a structured settlement?
A structured settlement is a financial arrangement where someone who won a legal claim — typically personal injury, workers' compensation, or wrongful death — receives their compensation in periodic payments over time rather than as a single lump sum. The payments are typically funded by an annuity purchased from an insurance company.
Is selling my settlement payments legal?
Yes. Every state has Structured Settlement Protection Act legislation that governs the sale of structured settlement payments. The process requires court approval, which is designed to protect you. Selling your future payments is a legal right you have as the settlement recipient.
How long does the process take?
The process typically takes 45 to 90 days from initial consultation to receipt of funds. The timeline is largely determined by court scheduling in your jurisdiction. We work to move as efficiently as the legal process allows.
Will I receive the full face value of my payments?
No — and any company that tells you otherwise is not being honest. We purchase your future payments at a discount to their face value (this is how all factoring companies work). We are completely transparent about the discount rate and what you will receive. The question to ask yourself is whether having the money now is worth the discount — for many people in specific circumstances, it clearly is.
Do I need my own attorney?
You are not required to have separate legal representation, though you are always welcome to consult your own attorney. Because our firm is led by a licensed attorney, the legal aspects of your transaction are handled professionally and with your interests in mind throughout the process.
