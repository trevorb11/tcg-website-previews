About ACE

Atlantic Coast Energy Group

Atlantic Coast Energy Group (ACE) is a digital diesel refueling company built around a simple mission: help businesses understand their fuel needs and provide refueling services that genuinely add value to their day-to-day operations.

We serve fleet owners, construction companies, railroads, transportation companies, and emergency service providers across the New York City metropolitan area and nationally — delivering reliable, professional onsite fueling that eliminates one of the most persistent operational headaches businesses face.
