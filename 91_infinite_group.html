Our Story

Design Meets Manufacturing

Infinite Group USA was founded in 2002 by Bernard Alloysius to address a gap in the market that the design industry knew well: there were design firms, and there were manufacturers — but few who truly excelled at both.

We built Infinite Group to be different. Based in Kearny, NJ, our facility brings together a dedicated team of designers and fabricators who work under one roof, speaking the same language, pursuing the same standard. The result is a manufacturing company that understands design intent — and a design capability grounded in what is actually achievable in production.

Over more than two decades, we have served retailers, luxury brands, architects, interior designers, and corporate clients who share a common expectation: that the finished product will be exactly what they imagined — or better.
