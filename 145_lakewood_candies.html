Our Story

A Family Passion Born in Lakewood, NJ

Founded in 2009, Lakewood Candies LLC began as a simple family dream — to share the joy of truly exceptional candy with the world. What started as a small operation out of Lakewood, New Jersey has grown into a beloved confectionery brand trusted by candy lovers across the country.

Our team of dedicated artisans, now nearly a dozen strong, hand-selects every ingredient and refines every recipe. From our kitchen to your home, each piece of candy carries the warmth of family tradition and the pride of craft.

👪

Family-Run Business

Every decision we make reflects our family values — quality, honesty, and a love for bringing people together through sweets.

🏭

Rooted in Lakewood, NJ

Proudly based at 1980 Swarthmore Ave, Lakewood — our community inspires everything we create.
