Founded by Industry
Professionals in 2013.

Daren Masters didn't open a school to fill seats. He opened one because he saw people who needed a real career path and couldn't find a training program that took them seriously. On December 6, 2013 — with Carol Aguilera as co-founder — he opened the doors in Fort Worth. More than a decade later, the school runs three campuses and has produced hundreds of certified phlebotomists working across Texas. Students on Yelp don't praise the building — they talk about confidence. About walking into a job interview knowing they could actually do the work.
