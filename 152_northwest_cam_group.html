What We Do

Comprehensive Security & AV Solutions

From a single camera to a full enterprise security ecosystem, we deliver end-to-end installations tailored to your specific environment and needs.

CCTV Installation

Full-spectrum analog and HD-over-coax CCTV system design and installation. Custom camera placement for maximum coverage with minimal blind spots.

IP Camera Systems

High-definition network IP cameras with remote viewing, cloud storage, and AI-powered motion detection. Crystal-clear footage day and night.

Access Control

Keycard, fob, biometric, and mobile-credential access control systems. Manage entry points, set permissions, and audit access logs in real time.

Video Analytics

Intelligent video analytics including people counting, license plate recognition, loitering alerts, and behavior analysis to elevate situational awareness.

AV Systems

Commercial audio-visual design and installation for conference rooms, lobbies, retail spaces, and entertainment environments. Seamless integration guaranteed.

Remote Monitoring

24/7 professional remote monitoring services with instant alert notifications. View your property from anywhere via our secure mobile and desktop platforms.

About Us

New York's Security Specialists

North West Cam Group is a New York-based security and AV technology company with over a decade of hands-on experience protecting homes, businesses, and institutions across the five boroughs and the wider tri-state area.

We combine the latest in surveillance technology with expert, professional installation — delivering systems that are reliable, scalable, and easy to manage. Whether you need a single camera for a brownstone or a multi-site enterprise rollout, our team has the expertise to handle it right.

  • Locally owned and operated in New York
  • Certified installers trained on leading hardware platforms
  • Turnkey solutions: design, installation, training, and support
  • Competitive pricing with transparent, no-surprise quotes
  • Ongoing service contracts and maintenance packages available
