About GKG

Built from the Ground Up.
For Purpose.

On May 12, 2002, Gretchen McCracken started Golden Key Group in her basement. She had a belief: that federal agencies deserved better human capital support, delivered by people who genuinely understood government missions. She was right.

Twenty-two years later, GKG has grown from that basement to a 350-person firm with headquarters in Reston, VA, a 20,000 square foot operations facility in Landover, MD, and offices across Kentucky and Alabama. Along the way, GKG has supported over 50 federal departments and agencies, including a thirteen-year partnership with the Environmental Protection Agency.

What hasn't changed is the belief that good people, well-supported and well-led, are what make government agencies succeed. That's what we do. That's who we are.

"We started this company because we believed federal agencies and the people who serve in them deserved better. Every contract we win is another chance to prove that belief right."

— Gretchen McCracken, CEO & Managing Partner

GKG Leadership

Gretchen McCracken

CEO and Managing Partner

Gretchen McCracken is one of the rare founders who still runs the company she started. Since founding GKG in 2002, she has guided the firm from a one-person consultancy to a 350-person organization trusted by over 50 federal agencies.

Her leadership has been recognized at the national level: she serves on the Professional Services Council Board of Directors and received the ESGR Patriot Award for her commitment to supporting employees who serve in the National Guard and Reserve.

Gretchen's approach is direct: great federal human capital work requires people who understand government from the inside. GKG hires and develops exactly those people, and Gretchen leads by that example every day.
