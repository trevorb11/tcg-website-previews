Our Story

Ninety Years of Keeping Our Word

In 1934, a small group of Franklin County residents decided their community needed a bank they could trust. They built United Bank of Union on the belief that neighbors should take care of neighbors, and that a bank should know your name, not just your account number.

Nine decades later, that belief hasn't wavered. We're still here, still locally owned, still making decisions in Union rather than some distant corporate office. When you walk in, you'll likely be greeted by someone who knows your family.

Today, with $240 million in assets and three branch locations, we hold the distinction of being among the top 10% of performing banks in the State of Missouri. We're proud of that ranking, but we're prouder of the relationships that got us there.

"A bank is only as good as its word, and we intend to keep ours to every family and business we serve."

Agricultural & USDA Loans

As the first USDA Business & Industrial Loan Program certified lender in the State of Missouri, we bring deep expertise to farming and agribusiness financing.

Missouri's First USDA B&I Certified Lender

United Bank of Union was the first bank in the State of Missouri to earn USDA Business & Industrial Loan Program certification. In plain terms: that means rural businesses and farms in Franklin County can access government-backed financing that many banks simply can't offer. It took years of work to earn that certification, and we've used it to help keep businesses and farms operating right here at home.

Our Community

More Than a Bank

United Bank of Union and its employees are valued partners with local schools, civic organizations, and volunteer groups throughout Franklin County. Our people don't just work here; they live here. They coach youth sports teams, serve on nonprofit boards, and show up when the community needs them.

That's not marketing talk. That's what happens when a bank is genuinely part of the place it serves. When you bank with us, your money stays in the community and helps the people around you.

We believe that's exactly how it should work, and we're not about to change it.
