Our Story

Built by a Utility Engineer. Trusted by the Industry.

Gerald Wagner spent years working in and around utility infrastructure before founding Wagner Technical Services in 1995. He knew the industry's gaps — the need for experienced, on-demand engineering resources that could scale with project demands without the overhead of a full-time hire.

Over three decades, that vision has been validated hundreds of times over. We've become one of the Northeast's most trusted engineering resources for electric and gas utilities, handling everything from transmission structure inspections and easement acquisition to plant accounting and custom software development.

We're not a generalist staffing firm that happens to do utility work. We're utility specialists — period. Our team knows electric transmission and distribution the way most firms know their own names, and that depth of expertise is what separates us from everyone else.

We built this company to give utilities the engineering muscle they need, exactly when they need it — without the bureaucracy that slows everything down.

Gerald Wagner — Founder, Wagner Technical Services

What We Do

Engineering Services for the Utility Sector

Three decades of experience concentrated in the services that utility companies need most — delivered by people who've dedicated their careers to this industry.

Transmission and Distribution Engineering

Design, analysis, and engineering support for electric transmission and distribution systems — from single-line diagrams to complex multi-circuit projects.

Field Inspection Services

Comprehensive inspection programs for transmission structures, distribution poles, and associated equipment. More than 790,000 structures inspected to date.

Easement and Right-of-Way

Easement acquisition, title research coordination, right-of-way document preparation, and stakeholder negotiation support for utility projects of all scales.

Work Order Preparation and Drafting

Detailed work order development, drafting services, and technical documentation for construction, maintenance, and upgrade projects.

Accounting Closeouts and Plant Accounting

Project closeout documentation, asset accounting, and plant ledger support — helping utilities maintain accurate, audit-ready financial records.

Estimating and Cost Analysis

Detailed cost estimating for utility construction projects, supporting planning, budgeting, and contractor procurement processes.

Proprietary Technology

Custom Software That Automates Your Workflow

One of the most impactful things Wagner Technical Services has done is build the tools the industry was missing. Our custom work order automation software was developed by utility engineers for utility engineers — and it has changed how our clients manage their field operations.

The software integrates directly with existing GIS and asset management systems, automating the creation of work orders that used to require hours of manual entry. The result is faster project delivery, fewer errors, and audit-ready documentation from day one.

  • Automated work order generation from field data and GIS inputs
  • Integrates with existing utility asset management platforms
  • Reduces manual data entry and associated errors
  • Full audit trail and documentation compliance
  • Scalable from single projects to enterprise-wide deployment
  • Developed and supported by our in-house engineering team
