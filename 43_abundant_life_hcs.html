Who We Are

A Trusted Partner for Texas Families for Over 15 Years

Mack Jones founded Abundant Life HCS after spending years working alongside individuals with intellectual disabilities and watching how often the systems meant to help them fell short — not from lack of funding, but from lack of genuine care. He started this organization because he believed people with disabilities deserve to be seen as whole people, not cases to be managed. That belief still shapes everything we do.

We are fully certified by the Texas Health and Human Services Commission and have served individuals across Texas through offices in Mount Pleasant, Dallas, Paris, and Texarkana. Our approach is always individualized — every person we serve has a unique story, unique strengths, and unique needs, and our care plans reflect that reality.

We are available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. When something comes up — whether it is a question about your loved one's care or an urgent situation that cannot wait — Mack and our team are reachable. We greatly encourage family involvement at every step. No one knows your loved one better than you do, and that insight makes every care plan we build stronger.

Our Programs

Three Paths to Individualized Support

Every family's situation is different. We offer three HCS program models so we can match the right level of support to each individual's needs and living situation.

Program One

Group Homes

Safe, warm homes where up to four individuals live together in a community setting. Each home is staffed by dedicated caregivers who support daily living, personal goals, and participation in community activities. Residents build friendships, develop independence, and live full lives within their neighborhoods.

Learn more →
Program Two

Foster / Companion Care

A foster or companion family welcomes an individual into their home, providing support within a nurturing family environment. This model is well-suited to individuals who thrive in a family setting and benefit from consistent one-on-one attention and a stable home life.

Learn more →
Program Three

Supported Home Living

For individuals living with family or independently, Supported Home Living provides in-home support services that help build daily living skills, encourage community participation, and ensure individuals have the assistance they need to thrive where they are most comfortable.

Getting Started

How the HCS Program Works

We know the process can feel overwhelming. Here is what to expect when you reach out to Abundant Life HCS.

1

Inquiry & First Conversation

Contact us by phone or email. We will listen to your situation, answer your initial questions, and help you understand whether the HCS program is the right fit for your loved one. There is no pressure — just an honest conversation about what is possible.

2

Needs Assessment

Our team conducts a thorough assessment of the individual's needs, strengths, goals, and living preferences. This assessment forms the foundation of a care plan that is truly individualized — not a generic template.

3

Care Planning with Your Family

We build the care plan together with the individual and their family. You bring knowledge of your loved one that no one else has. We bring clinical expertise and program knowledge. Together, we create a plan that reflects both.

4

Services Begin

Once approved and planned, services begin in the care setting that best meets your loved one's needs — whether a group home, foster family, or in-home support. Our case managers stay closely involved from the start.

5

Ongoing Support & Adjustment

Care needs evolve over time, and we evolve with them. Regular reviews, open communication with families, and responsive case management ensure that the care we provide always fits the person receiving it.

Common Questions Answered

What is the HCS program?

The Home and Community-based Services (HCS) program is a Texas Medicaid waiver program that provides services and supports to people with intellectual disabilities who live with their families, in their own homes, or in small community settings. The goal is to support individuals to live as independently as possible in the community of their choice.

Who qualifies for HCS services?

To qualify for the HCS program, an individual must have an intellectual disability, be eligible for Medicaid, and meet certain functional requirements established by the Texas Health and Human Services Commission. An official assessment will determine eligibility. Contact us and we will help you understand whether your loved one may qualify.

How long is the HCS waiting list?

The HCS waiver program has a waitlist that can vary significantly in length depending on where the individual lives and when they applied. Wait times can range from several months to several years. We encourage families to get their loved one on the waitlist as early as possible and to contact us about current status and any available alternatives in the meantime.

What does HCS cost for families?

HCS services are funded through Medicaid, so most families do not pay out-of-pocket costs for program services. The individual must be Medicaid-eligible to participate. We can help your family understand the financial aspects of the program during an initial conversation.

How do I get started?

The best first step is to reach out to us directly by phone or through the contact form below. We will have a conversation about your loved one's situation, answer your questions, and help you understand what next steps look like. There is no commitment — just a conversation with people who understand what your family is going through.
