Investment Thesis

We Back Founders at the Frontier

The most interesting companies of the next decade will not be purely digital. They will be things you can hold, install, and use — products that also connect, sense, and adapt. Wakestream was built to find these founders early.

Michigan has been building physical things for over a century. The design sensibility, the manufacturing know-how, the problem-solving culture — it is still here. Wakestream is rooted in that heritage while looking squarely at what is next. We are a generalist early-stage fund with a sharp focus on founders who understand both atoms and bits.

We take first meetings seriously. We move with conviction. And once we invest, we stay engaged — as a real partner, not a passive cap table entry.

Investment Criteria

What We Look For

We are generalists by design, but the founders who find the best fit with Wakestream share a few things in common.

Physical Meets Digital

Products that live in the real world and connect to the internet. IoT, smart hardware, connected consumer goods, industrial sensors. If you can hold it and it also thinks, we are interested.

Defensible Hardware

Manufacturing know-how, industrial design, and supply chain relationships that are not easy to replicate. The best hardware startups build moats before they need them.

Visionary Founders

Builders who understand both the physical and digital domains deeply. People who can ship something people want to touch while also building the software layer that makes it remarkable.

Our Home

Built in Michigan

Michigan has been building physical things for over a century. The auto industry brought world-class manufacturing engineering, industrial design talent, and a culture that knows how to take an idea from a drawing and make it into a thing you can actually use.

That culture did not go away. It evolved. Today, Michigan founders are some of the most capable hardware builders in the country, and they have a built-in understanding of manufacturing that pure software investors simply cannot offer.

"We are not a coastal fund wearing Michigan as a costume. We live here, invest here, and believe the most interesting hardware founders in the country are building right here in the Midwest."
Our Process

How We Work With Founders

We try to be the investor we would have wanted ourselves. That means honest, fast, and genuinely engaged from the first conversation.

01

First Conversation

Tell us what you are building. We listen, ask questions, and give real feedback — not a form rejection.

02

Due Diligence

We dig into the product, the team, the market. We move fast and tell you where we stand as we go.

03

Term Sheet

Straightforward terms. We do not bury founders in legal complexity. Clean documents and fair economics.

04

Active Partnership

After we invest, we stay in your corner — on hiring, next rounds, manufacturing questions, and anything else you need.
