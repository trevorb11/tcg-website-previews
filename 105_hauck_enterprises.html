What We Do

Complete Cemetery Care

Hauck Enterprises provides the full range of cemetery maintenance services — as a single-source contractor who manages everything so you do not have to.

Cemetery Lawn Care

Regular mowing, edging, trimming, and grounds upkeep that keeps cemetery grounds looking well-maintained and dignified throughout the growing season. We work around headstones and grave markers with the care and precision that these sites require.

Burial Maintenance

Grave site preparation, settlings, sodding, and ongoing grave maintenance to ensure individual sites remain level, clean, and properly cared for. Our crews treat every grave with the respect that families and loved ones expect.

Single-Source Contractor

When you work with Hauck, you have one call to make. We coordinate all maintenance functions, ensure consistent quality across the entire grounds, and eliminate the management burden of juggling multiple vendors. One contract. One call. Done right.

The Trust Families Place in These Grounds

We have spent over three decades building a company around the understanding that this work is different from other landscaping and maintenance work. Our crews know it. Our supervisors know it. And the cemetery administrators who have worked with us for years know it too.

Hauck Enterprises brings skilled workers, a proven performance history, and a rigorous quality control program to every location we serve. We hold ourselves accountable to a standard that reflects the dignity these grounds deserve.
