Our Story

More Than a Bank.
A Community Partner.

Texana Bank has been a cornerstone of East Texas banking since 1914. Founded by local leaders who believed their community deserved a financial institution that understood local needs and kept local money working locally, we've held to that founding vision for over a century.

Today we serve families and businesses across the Longview area and East Texas with a full range of personal and commercial banking products, real estate lending, and investment services — all delivered with the attentiveness and accountability that distinguishes a community bank from a national chain.

"We believe that when a community's bank thrives, the whole community thrives. That's been our philosophy since day one, and it guides every decision we make."

When you call Texana Bank, you reach someone who lives and works in your community. When you apply for a loan, the decision is made locally by bankers who know East Texas. That's not just good service — it's what community banking is supposed to be.
