Who We Are

The Right Surface Changes Everything

ASMI Stone and Tile LLC was built on a straightforward belief: fabricators, designers, and builders deserve a distribution partner who makes their job easier, not harder. That means having the right materials in stock, providing accurate specifications, and delivering product reliably — every time.

We distribute premium surface materials across the Midwest from our showrooms in St. Louis and Kansas, where clients can experience our materials firsthand — the texture, the finish, the scale — before making a decision. No guessing from a small sample chip.

Ronald Clair and the ASMI team have built relationships with producers around the world to offer materials that range from accessible and practical to rare and extraordinary. Our Vault Collection, manufactured with Italian Breton Stone Technology, represents the highest standard of engineered slab available in our region.

What Sets Our Distribution Apart

01

NSF & Greenguard Gold Certified

Our Vault Collection carries NSF and Greenguard Gold certification, meeting the most rigorous standards for food safety and indoor air quality — essential for kitchen and commercial applications.

02

Italian Breton Stone Technology

The Vault Collection is manufactured using Breton Stone Technology — the Italian engineering process that defines the gold standard in engineered slab quality, consistency, and durability.

03

Locally Stocked Inventory

We maintain substantial inventory at both of our Midwest locations, which means the material you select today is available for fulfillment on your timeline — not whenever a container arrives from overseas.

04

Our team understands surface materials from a technical and aesthetic standpoint. We can assist with material specification, tolerance requirements, fabrication considerations, and installation compatibility.
