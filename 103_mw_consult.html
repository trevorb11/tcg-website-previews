Who We Are

Built on Service. Driven by Expertise.

Mountain West Consulting was founded by Andrew Schaaf, PMP — a commissioned officer with nearly thirty years of military service and a career spent deep inside the infrastructure that powers American industry. Andrew has not just managed energy projects from an office. He has worked inside power plants, commissioned complex systems, and executed modifications in active operating facilities.

That experience shapes everything about how MWC approaches projects. We bring accountability and precision to every engagement, integrating directly with your operations rather than working around them. We understand what it means when something has to be right — not just on paper, but in the field.

As a Service-Disabled Veteran-Owned Small Business, MWC also provides set-aside contracting advantages for qualifying federal and utility clients. We take the designation seriously — it reflects the culture of the firm.

Core Services

What We Deliver

MWC offers four core workstreams designed to fit the full range of infrastructure project delivery needs — from full project management to focused owner's representation.

Project Management

Workstream PM

End-to-end project management for electric utility and industrial infrastructure projects. We own the schedule, budget, and scope — and we integrate directly with your engineering, operations, and construction teams to deliver on all three.

Construction Management

Workstream CM

On-site construction management for complex infrastructure builds. Our team is boots-on-the-ground, managing contractor performance, quality control, safety compliance, and daily progress against the baseline schedule.

Owner Advocacy

Owner's Representation

When you need an experienced advocate in your corner to manage designers and contractors, review submittals, evaluate change orders, and protect your project budget — MWC serves as your Owner's Representative with the technical depth to make a real difference.

Specialized Operations

Outage & Turnaround Management

Planned outages and turnarounds at power plants and industrial facilities are high-stakes, time-compressed events. MWC has the experience to plan, coordinate, and execute outage work — minimizing downtime and keeping every trade on track.

Our Approach

How We Work

Military service teaches you something that most project management theory misses: outcomes depend on people, and people follow clear expectations, consistent accountability, and leadership that earns trust every day. MWC brings that culture to every project.

01

Integrate With Your Team

We embed with your operations, engineering, and leadership teams rather than working alongside them. We function as an extension of your organization with external accountability.

02

Set Clear Baselines

Every project starts with a scope, schedule, and budget baseline that every stakeholder understands and agrees to. No surprises starts with no ambiguity.

03

Report Accurately and Promptly

We tell you what is happening — good and bad — in real time. Difficult information delivered early saves projects. Difficult information delivered late costs them.

04

Deliver

Our reputation is built on one thing: the projects we manage get completed on time and within budget. We are accountable to that standard on every engagement.
