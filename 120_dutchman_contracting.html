Who We Are

A Contracting Company Built on Reputation

Dutchman Contracting Corp has been a trusted name in New York area construction for over three decades. Under the leadership of Tom, our president who has driven the company's growth since its early days, Dutchman has built its reputation job by job — doing what we said we'd do, when we said we'd do it, at the price we agreed on.

That sounds simple, but in the construction industry it's rarer than it should be. We've built long-term relationships with commercial clients, property managers, and developers who come back to us again and again because they know what they're going to get: quality craftsmanship, transparent communication, and a finished project that stands the test of time.

Our team includes experienced construction professionals who have run their own companies and brought that entrepreneurial accountability to every project they work on. When you hire Dutchman, you're getting people who take personal ownership of the work.

  • On Time, Under Budget We set realistic timelines and hold ourselves to them.
  • Craftsman Quality We don't cut corners. Every detail reflects our 30-year reputation.
  • Clear Communication We return calls, give updates, and flag issues before they become problems.
