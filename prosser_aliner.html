Why Aliner

Five Reasons I Keep Selling These Things

Look, I've sold every kind of RV out there. Here's why Aliners keep flying off the lot.

30-Second Setup. I've Timed It.

No cranks, no poles, no wrestling with canvas in the rain. You unlatch it, the A-frame pops up, you're done. I've watched first-timers do it on their first try.

Lighter Than a Jet Ski

Most of these weigh less than a jet ski. Your SUV can handle it. A CR-V, a RAV4, even some sedans. No diesel truck required. That changes everything about weekend trips.

Hard Walls, Real Roof

This isn't a canvas tent on wheels. It's a hardwall camper with real insulation. When it rains at 2 AM, you're not lying there listening to canvas flap and wondering if you're going to get wet.

Fits in Your Garage

When it's folded down, an Aliner is about as tall as your car. It fits in a standard garage. Your HOA will never know. No paying for storage, no driving to a lot every Friday.

Road Trip Ready

Peninsula State Park, the Apostle Islands, Door County, wherever. Hook up Friday after work and you're at the campsite by dinner. No reservations, no hotels, no hassle.

Honest Talk

Is an Aliner Right for You?

I'd rather help you figure this out now than have you find out on your first trip. Here's the straight answer.

✓ Great If You...

  • + Want something your SUV, minivan, or crossover can tow without breaking a sweat
  • + Take weekend trips, long weekends, or week-long road trips as a couple
  • + Love national parks but hate setting up camp for an hour
  • + Want hard walls and a real roof, not a tent on wheels
  • + Are a first-time RV buyer who doesn't want to spend $60K on a trial run
  • + Need to store your camper in the garage or driveway without upsetting the neighbors
  • + Want to spend more time at the campfire and less time at the gas pump

— Maybe Not If You...

  • - Need to sleep 4+ people comfortably every trip
  • - Want a big living room to hang out in during rainy days
  • - Plan to live in your RV full-time or for months at a stretch
  • - Need a full kitchen with a big fridge and oven
  • - Travel with large dogs that need floor space
  • - Want slide-outs, recliners, and a home theater setup
