Our Story

Built on Two Decades of Pacific Northwest Fieldwork

Rivers Edge Environmental Services has been a fixture in the Puget Sound region for over 20 years. Founded by Paul Kemp — who brought a Midwesterner's work ethic and a deep respect for the Pacific Northwest landscape to Washington — the company built its reputation on doing difficult environmental work right.

We specialize in remediation and hazardous materials services, working with federal agencies, municipalities, developers, and industrial clients to clean up contaminated sites, manage hazardous materials safely, and bring properties back to productive use.

We operate across Washington, Oregon, Idaho, and Montana, with regulatory expertise in each state's environmental compliance requirements. As a member of the Northwest Environmental Business Council, we're a vetted, peer-recognized firm in the regional environmental services community.

What We Do

Full-Service Environmental Solutions

From initial assessment through final cleanup, we manage the full lifecycle of environmental remediation projects. Every project gets the same attention to safety, regulatory compliance, and quality of work.

🌍

Site Remediation

Soil and groundwater cleanup using proven, EPA-compliant methods. We assess contamination sources, design remediation approaches, and execute cleanup to meet regulatory standards — then document everything for closure.

⚠

Hazardous Materials Management

Certified identification, handling, packaging, transport coordination, and disposal of hazardous materials including asbestos, lead paint, petroleum products, and industrial chemicals. Safe at every step.

📋

Environmental Assessment

Phase I and Phase II Environmental Site Assessments for due diligence, regulatory compliance, and pre-construction requirements. Clear, thorough reporting that meets lending, agency, and legal standards.

🏗

Construction-Related Remediation

Environmental remediation integrated directly into construction and development projects. We coordinate with your project team to manage contamination discovery, regulatory reporting, and cleanup without stopping your schedule.

🚨

Emergency Response

Rapid-deployment response for spills, releases, and environmental incidents. When time matters, our crews mobilize quickly to contain and remediate — minimizing environmental impact and regulatory exposure.

📄

Regulatory Compliance Support

Guidance on WA Department of Ecology, Oregon DEQ, Idaho DEQ, and EPA regulatory requirements. We help you understand your obligations and navigate the permitting and reporting process.
