Our Story

Born from a Love of Wine Country

There is something special about wine country that resists being rushed. The morning light through the vines. The quiet of a barrel room. A glass of something remarkable shared without a schedule pressing down on you.

When Michael Graham founded Napa Valley Tours & Transportation more than 20 years ago, he built it around that feeling. We are not a bus tour. We are not a shuttle. We are your personal host in Napa Valley — the kind of local connection that turns a day of wine tasting into a memory that lasts a lifetime.

Our team knows the wineries, the back roads, the hidden gems, and the storied estates. We have built relationships with the people who make the wine. That is the difference between visiting Napa and truly experiencing it.
