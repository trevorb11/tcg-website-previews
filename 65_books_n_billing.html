Our Story

The Bookkeeper You Always Wished You'd Found

Karen Warr started Books N Billing because she saw too many great businesses struggling with their books. Too many small business owners spending Saturday nights hunched over receipts instead of actually living their lives. She decided to do something about it.

Since 2012, Books N Billing has been the financial partner small businesses in Central Florida and across the country rely on. We're not a big firm with rotating staff and generic packages. We're a dedicated team of degreed accountants who actually care about your business — because we've built one ourselves.

We specialize in QuickBooks and small business bookkeeping. We keep your records clean, your filings on time, and your financial picture clear — so you can focus on what you're actually good at.

Meet the Team

Meet Karen Warr

President & Founder | Books N Billing Inc

Karen Warr has spent over a decade making sure small business owners in Central Florida — and across the country — never have to lose sleep over their books again. She started Books N Billing in 2012 with a simple premise: small businesses deserve professional bookkeeping, and professional bookkeeping doesn't have to cost a fortune or feel impersonal.

A woman veteran and business owner herself, Karen understands the pressure of running a small business firsthand. She and her partner Dean Warr lead a team of degreed accountants with over 60 combined years of experience — people who genuinely enjoy helping businesses get financially organized and stay that way.

Karen's specialties include QuickBooks consulting, payroll, and working with businesses that need bookkeeping cleanup before they're ready to face their CPA. She works with clients locally in Melbourne, Florida and virtually across the country.
