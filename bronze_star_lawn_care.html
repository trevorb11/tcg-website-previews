Our Story

Mission-Driven Quality from a Veteran Who Cares

Bronze Star Complete Lawn Care and Home Solutions was founded in 2022 by Robert C. Ford, a service-disabled veteran with a clear mission: to bring the same discipline, precision, and commitment to excellence he learned in the military to the homes and properties of his community.

With over 17 years of hands-on experience in the lawn care and home improvement industry, Robert built Bronze Star from the ground up. Today, the company fields a team of 14 skilled professionals who serve residential and commercial clients across Phenix City, Columbus, Fort Moore, and the surrounding region.

The name "Bronze Star" is a direct reference to military service and the values it instills: integrity, courage, and an unwavering commitment to getting the job done right. Every project, whether it is a weekly lawn mowing or a full kitchen remodel, receives that same standard of care.
