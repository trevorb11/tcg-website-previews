About Us

Built from Service. Built for Service.

Pivotal Point LLC was founded in 2008 in Falls Church, Virginia — right in the heart of the federal contracting corridor. Dokmai Webster built this firm from the ground up with a single standard: deliver technically sound IT and professional services that agencies can actually count on. We earned our reputation one successful contract at a time.

We hold both SDVOSB and EDWOSB certifications. These are not just credentials on a page. They reflect who we are, where we came from, and the values we bring to every engagement. Service is not a business strategy for us. It is how we were built.

From the VA to NASA to DISA, we have earned the trust of agencies that simply cannot afford anything less than reliable. Dokmai and the team bring that same standard to every task order, every deliverable, and every contract we sign.
