Who We Are

Engineering Excellence on the Ramp

Marshell Dills founded B GSE Group LLC with a conviction born from years on the ramp: ground support equipment is not a commodity — it is a critical system that airports, military installations, and aviation operations depend on every single day. When ground power fails or a PCA unit goes down, aircraft cannot load, flights delay, and costs escalate rapidly. Marshell built BGSE Group to ensure that never happens.

Under Marshell's leadership, our team has grown into a group of airline pilots, retired military personnel, and aircraft utility experts who bring more than 160 years of combined experience to every project. Based in Huntersville, North Carolina, we design, manufacture, and install turnkey GSE systems for terminals, ramps, and hangars across the globe.

We have completed over 300 military contracts and work with the world's leading aerospace manufacturers and carriers — delivering systems that reduce fuel consumption, cut capital maintenance costs, and increase ramp efficiency from day one.
