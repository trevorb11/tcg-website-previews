About Tricor

National Scale. Local Execution.

Tricor Contracting was founded in 2014 by Josh Fancher in Scottsdale, Arizona, with a clear vision: become the most reliable construction partner for institutional investors operating in the single-family rental and build-to-rent space. A decade later, that vision has been realized at a scale few expected.

With over 22,000 homes renovated, more than $5 billion in assets managed, and operations across nine major markets, Tricor has built a track record that speaks plainly. We doubled our expected annual growth in 2022. We hold BBB accreditation. And our clients come back because we do what we say we're going to do, on time and on budget.

From Phoenix to Raleigh, Nashville to Salt Lake City, Tricor combines the discipline of an institutional operator with the accountability of a firm that puts its name on every house it touches.

Who We Serve

Built for Institutional Investors

Tricor's client base is institutional: large portfolio owners, private equity real estate firms, REITs, and family offices that own or are acquiring single-family rental assets at scale. We speak that language, and we understand what matters to these clients.

  • Long-term profitability focus: We build and renovate for durability and tenant retention, not just lowest upfront cost.
  • Risk reduction: Our processes, quality controls, and reporting systems reduce execution risk across large, dispersed portfolios.
  • Client specification adherence: We build to your specs, not our preferences. Your standards are the standards.
  • Multi-market scalability: One partner across nine markets. Consistent quality, consistent reporting, consistent accountability.

Our Process

How We Deliver

A disciplined process, repeated at scale. From scope to handover, every project follows the same accountable steps.

01

Scope Assessment

Detailed property walk-through and scope documentation aligned to client specifications.

02

Budget & Timeline

Fixed-cost bid and milestone schedule reviewed and approved before any work begins.

03

Construction

04

Quality Inspection

Independent quality control walk with punch list resolution before delivery.

05

Turnover

Clean handover documentation, photos, and warranty information delivered to investor.

Leadership

Josh Fancher

Founder & Chief Operating Officer

Josh Fancher co-founded Tricor in 2014 out of a conviction that institutional real estate investors deserved a construction partner that operated with the same discipline they brought to acquisitions. That conviction shaped everything at Tricor: the processes, the reporting, the emphasis on long-term profitability over short-term cost-cutting.

Under Josh's leadership, Tricor has grown from a regional operation to a nine-market national platform with 22,000+ homes completed and more than $5 billion in assets managed. The company doubled its expected annual growth in 2022, a result that Arizona Foothills Magazine recognized in naming Josh a "Trendsetter to Know."

Josh remains deeply involved in the operational decisions that drive Tricor's performance and in relationships with the firm's key institutional partners.

"Tricor's success comes from treating every house like it's the only one on your schedule. That discipline doesn't scale naturally, but we've built systems that make it possible."

— Josh Fancher, Founder & COO, featured in Arizona Foothills Magazine
