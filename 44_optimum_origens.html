Who We Are

Technology Should Work for You — Not the Other Way Around

Bjorn Campbell founded Optimum Origens in 2013 with a frustration that every small business owner understands: technology was supposed to make things easier, but for most SMBs it created more problems than it solved. Unreliable networks, unclear security posture, no one answering when something breaks — Optimum Origens was built to fix that.

Based in West Palm Beach, Florida, we serve small and mid-sized businesses across healthcare, legal, finance, education, and government. Our team brings over 36 years of combined experience in large enterprise IT environments — which means our clients get enterprise-grade thinking at a size that actually makes sense for their business.

We do not just respond to problems. We prevent them. That proactive mindset is what separates a true strategic IT partner from just another vendor you call when things go wrong.

What We Do

Comprehensive IT Services for Growing Businesses

From helpdesk support to full cybersecurity programs, we provide the technology services that keep West Palm Beach businesses running smoothly and safely.

Managed IT Services

Fully managed IT support for your business — hardware, software, users, and infrastructure. Predictable monthly pricing, unlimited helpdesk support, and complete technology oversight from a team that knows your environment.

Network Monitoring & Management

24/7 monitoring of your network infrastructure to catch problems before they become outages. We manage switches, routers, firewalls, and Wi-Fi with proactive alerting and response protocols in place.

Cloud Computing Solutions

Cloud migration strategy, deployment, and ongoing management. Whether you are moving to Microsoft 365, Azure, or a hybrid environment, we handle the complexity and ensure the transition does not disrupt your operations.

Cybersecurity & Endpoint Security

Comprehensive security protection for your business — endpoint detection, email security, multi-factor authentication, vulnerability scanning, and employee security awareness training. Threats are real; your defenses should be too.

Data Backup & Recovery

Automated, tested backups for your critical business data with clear recovery time objectives. When ransomware hits or hardware fails, you need to know your data is safe and that you can be back up and running quickly.

Proactive IT Support & Helpdesk

Fast, friendly helpdesk support for your team — via phone, email, or remote session. Our technicians know your environment and resolve issues with minimal disruption to your workday. Real people, real answers, real fast.
