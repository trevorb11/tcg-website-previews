About Iron Systems

Built for the Demands of Enterprise IT

Founded in 2002, Iron Systems has grown from a focused U.S. technology company into a global OEM and ODM partner with offices in over 20 countries, 4,000+ skilled engineers, and 600+ warehouses worldwide.

We design, build, and deploy custom server appliances and embedded systems platforms for the world's most demanding environments. Our model is simple: we handle the complexity of custom server engineering and global logistics so that OEM partners, cloud providers, and enterprise customers can focus on what they do best.

Iron Systems is a Dell OEM Partner and an Intel-certified partner. Those relationships, combined with our own engineering capabilities, give us the flexibility to build solutions that off-the-shelf hardware simply can't match.
