Full-Spectrum Services

One Partner. The Entire Journey.
Start to Shelf.

Most founders burning through three separate vendors — a design shop, an engineering firm, and a manufacturing contact who do not talk to each other. We handle all of it under one roof, which saves time, money, and a lot of difficult conversations you should not have to manage.

Product Strategy & Ideation

Market research, competitive analysis, concept development, and feasibility studies. For clients who have an idea and need a clear path forward before committing to development.

Industrial Design

Form, ergonomics, aesthetics, and materials selection. We deliver CAD models, photorealistic renders, and concept visualization to bring your product to life before a single part is made.

Engineering & Prototyping

Mechanical engineering, DFM analysis, rapid prototyping via 3D printing and CNC, and functional testing. We validate before you invest in tooling.

Manufacturing Sourcing

Domestic and overseas manufacturing partner network. Supplier vetting, tooling oversight, quality control, and first article inspection. We manage the relationship so you do not have to.

Regulatory & Compliance

FCC, UL, CE, FDA (where applicable), and safety certification support. We prepare the documentation and coordinate with testing labs to ensure your product reaches market without surprises.

Product Launch Support

Retail channel preparation, Amazon and e-commerce listing optimization, launch inventory planning, and packaging design. We help you land on shelves and in carts.

Our Process

A Proven Path from Idea to Production

Every engagement starts with a signed NDA. Then we walk through six structured phases that give you visibility and control at every stage.

Your IP. Always. No Exceptions.

Every engagement starts with a mutual NDA — before you share a single detail. Every file we produce, every CAD model, every supplier relationship and manufacturing spec belongs to you. We do not retain rights to anything we create for your project.

Start Your Project
1
Phase 1

Discovery & Brief

We learn everything about your product vision, market, technical constraints, and budget. NDA is executed. Goals and success metrics are defined together before any work begins.

Deliverable: Project Brief + Signed NDA
2
Phase 2

Concept Development

Industrial design explorations, competitive benchmarking, and initial 3D sketches. You receive multiple concept directions before we narrow to one path for development.

Deliverable: Concept Renders + Design Brief
3
Phase 3

Design Refinement

Detailed CAD modeling, materials selection, ergonomic refinement, and DFM optimization. We work closely with you through revision cycles to arrive at a production-ready design.

Deliverable: Final CAD Files + BOM Draft
4
Phase 4

Prototyping & Testing

Functional prototypes built via 3D printing, CNC, or soft tooling. We run performance tests, gather feedback, and validate against specifications before moving to tooling.

Deliverable: Functional Prototype + Test Report
5
Phase 5

Manufacturing Preparation

Supplier selection and vetting, tooling development, quality control protocols, and production specs documentation. We manage factory relationships on your behalf throughout.

Deliverable: Manufacturing Package + Supplier Contracts
6
Phase 6

Production Launch

First article inspection, regulatory certification coordination, packaging finalization, and channel readiness support. Your product ships with confidence.

Deliverable: Final Product + Full Documentation Package
