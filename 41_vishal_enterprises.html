Our Story

35 Years of Craftsmanship in the Heart of Fashion

Mahesh Moorjani founded Vishal Enterprises in 1989 with a simple belief: every designer deserves access to world-class manufacturing expertise, not just the biggest brands. From a small operation in the New York garment district, the company has grown into a vertically integrated fashion partner serving clients across the spectrum — from emerging labels to household names.

Operating under the Natasha International brand for fabric and textile sourcing, Vishal Enterprises offers the rare combination of deep pattern-making expertise, sustainable fabric sourcing, and both domestic and overseas production under one roof. Certified as a Minority Business Enterprise and Small Business Enterprise in New Jersey, we bring authentic craftsmanship and genuine partnership to every project.

What We Do

Full-Service Fashion
Manufacturing & Consulting

From pattern to production, we handle every step of bringing your collection to market — with the attention to detail that has made us trusted by the industry's best.

Pattern Making & Grading

Precision pattern development from designer sketches, with full grading across size runs. Rapid turnaround without sacrificing accuracy or fit quality.

Fabric & Trim Sourcing

Expert guidance through sustainable and conventional fabric selection. Through Natasha International, we source trims and textiles that match your design vision and budget.

Sample Making

From first proto to final pre-production sample, our skilled sample room team brings your designs to life quickly — so you can get to fittings and approvals without delay.

Production Services

Flexible domestic and overseas manufacturing from small capsule runs to large-scale production orders. Full quality oversight at every stage, regardless of location.

Digital Fabric Printing

Custom digital printing directly on fabric for unique patterns, exclusive prints, and limited-edition designs. Vibrant color and precision reproduction from your artwork.

Fashion Consulting

Strategic guidance from concept through commercialization. Whether you're launching your first line or reorienting an established brand, our team provides clear, experienced direction.
