Our Story

More Than Nine Decades. Same Commitment to You.

McCarthy Insurance opened its doors in Hawthorne, New York in 1930. Through world wars, economic cycles, and a constantly changing insurance market, one thing has never changed: our commitment to putting clients first and navigating the complexity of insurance on their behalf.

We work for you — not for any insurance company. That independence means our only obligation is finding you the right coverage at the best possible price.

As an independent broker, we have relationships with dozens of leading carriers — including Travelers, Chubb, Progressive, and Main Street America. That means we can shop your coverage across the market and deliver options a single-company agent simply can't offer.

We're proud of our roots in Hawthorne and our deep ties to Westchester County. When you call McCarthy Insurance, you talk to a neighbor — someone who understands this community because they live in it.

Giving Back

We Believe in the Communities We Protect

McCarthy Insurance is proud to support causes that reflect the values of our clients and our community. Protecting people extends beyond insurance policies.

Tunnel to Towers Foundation

McCarthy Insurance donates to the Tunnel to Towers Foundation, which provides mortgage-free homes to the families of fallen first responders and catastrophically injured veterans. We believe in honoring those who protect others — it's close to what we do every day.

One Tree Planted

Through our partnership with One Tree Planted, McCarthy Insurance contributes to global reforestation efforts — helping restore forests, support biodiversity, and leave the environment better than we found it. Small actions, lasting impact.
