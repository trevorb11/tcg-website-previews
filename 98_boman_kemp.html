Why Egress Matters

Life Safety Starts Below Grade

Egress windows are not just a building feature — they are a life-safety requirement. Every finished or habitable basement needs a properly sized window that allows occupants to escape in an emergency. Getting it wrong is not an option.

The International Residential Code (IRC) sets minimum requirements for egress window size, well width, and clear opening dimensions. Boman-Kemp products are engineered specifically to meet those requirements so builders, contractors, and homeowners never have to guess.

Since 1967, we have been the manufacturer that building professionals trust to get this right the first time. Our complete systems ship matched and ready to install, eliminating the guesswork that comes with piecing together components from different suppliers.

IRC Egress Code

Egress windows must provide a minimum clear opening of 5.7 square feet, minimum height of 24 inches, minimum width of 20 inches, and a maximum sill height of 44 inches from the floor. Boman-Kemp systems are designed to satisfy these requirements.

Window Wells

Code-compliant escape ladders for window wells deeper than 44 inches. Required by IRC for accessible emergency exit and designed for secure attachment to the well wall.
