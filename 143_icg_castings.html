What We Do

Core Capabilities

From prototype to high-volume production, ICG Castings provides the full spectrum of aluminum die-casting services under one roof.

Aluminum Die-Casting

High-pressure aluminum die-casting for complex geometries, tight tolerances, and superior surface finish. Designed for automotive durability and performance standards.

Tooling & Die Design

In-house tooling engineering and die design capabilities. We build, maintain, and optimize dies to ensure consistent part quality throughout the production lifecycle.

Precision Machining

Secondary CNC machining operations to achieve final dimensional requirements, ensuring cast parts meet strict automotive assembly specifications.

Prototype Development

Rapid prototype casting services to validate designs early in the development cycle. Reduce time-to-launch with fast iteration and engineering collaboration.

Quality Inspection

CMM measurement, X-ray inspection, leak testing, and visual quality audits at every stage. Zero-defect commitment backed by documented process control.

High-Volume Production

Scalable production capacity to support automotive supply chain demands. Consistent cycle times, optimized process flows, and on-time delivery performance.

Who We Are

Quality Commitment

Built on Precision & Process

Every part that leaves our facility is backed by documented quality control, traceable data, and inspection records that meet automotive supply chain standards.

CMM Dimensional Inspection

Coordinate measuring machine verification against customer GD&T requirements at first-article and production sampling.

X-Ray & Porosity Testing

Radiographic inspection to detect internal voids, porosity, and inclusions in safety-critical and structural castings.

Pressure & Leak Testing

Functional leak testing for fluid-carrying and pressure-retaining cast components to confirm seal integrity.

Process FMEA & Control Plans

Risk-based process design with documented control plans, reaction instructions, and PPAP-aligned documentation packages.

Statistical Process Control

Real-time SPC monitoring of critical casting parameters to maintain process stability and detect drift before nonconformance.

Traceability & PPAP

Full part traceability from melt heat to finished shipment, with PPAP submission capability to automotive Tier 1 and OEM standards.
