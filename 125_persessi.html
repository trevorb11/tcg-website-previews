About Persessi

Mission-Driven.
Results-Focused.

Persessi LLC was founded by Brian Ingram, a military veteran whose service instilled a deep commitment to precision, reliability, and getting the mission done right the first time. Today, we bring that same discipline to unified communications and technology deployment for businesses across the country.

Based in the Chapel Hill/Durham area of North Carolina, our team specializes in designing, implementing, and managing communication systems that help organizations collaborate more effectively, reduce costs, and scale with confidence.

Whether your team is in a single office, across multiple locations, or entirely remote, we build communication infrastructure that keeps people connected and projects on track.
