Meet Your Agent

Your Neighbor. Your Agent. Since Day One.

Carlos Rexach opened La Cinco Agency on N. 5th Street because he saw neighbors struggling to navigate insurance in a system that was not built with them in mind. He wanted to change that — give working families in North Philadelphia the same access to affordable, well-explained coverage that everyone deserves. More than two decades later, he is still here, still answering his own phone, still getting his clients better rates.
