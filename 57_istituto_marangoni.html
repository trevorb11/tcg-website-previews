Our Heritage

Ninety Years of Fashion Excellence. Now in Miami.

Founded in Milan in 1935, Istituto Marangoni has been shaping the careers of fashion and design professionals for nearly a century. Through campuses in Milan, London, Paris, Shanghai, Mumbai, and beyond, the school has established itself as one of the world's leading institutions for fashion education.

Miami is our tenth campus and our first in the United States. Situated in the Miami Design District, one of the world's most vibrant luxury and design destinations, our Miami campus brings the Marangoni tradition of excellence to the Americas with a distinctly forward-looking spirit.

Our seven-story campus houses cutting-edge classrooms, design studios, an atelier, a professional photoshoot studio, and collaborative spaces designed to mirror real creative industry environments. You don't just study here. You practice.

Our Campus

Seven Floors. One Creative World.

Our Miami campus is not just a school; it's an environment designed to make you better at what you love. Located in the Miami Design District, surrounded by the world's premier luxury brands and design institutions, the building itself is an inspiration.

Every space has been built with creative work in mind. Students move from theory-based studios to hands-on ateliers to professional photoshoot environments, experiencing what it actually feels like to work in the industry before they enter it.
