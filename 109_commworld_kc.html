Still Standing When Others Couldn't

In an industry that has seen hundreds of providers come and go, CommWorld KC remains — serving Kansas City businesses with the same reliability that built our reputation from day one.

What We Do

Complete Business Communications

From your phone system to your security cameras, CommWorld KC handles the full communications stack so you can focus on your business.

VoIP Phone Systems

Modern hosted and on-premise voice-over-IP phone systems designed for the way Kansas City businesses actually work — reliable, scalable, and easy to manage.

  • Cloud PBX and on-premise hybrid options
  • Mobile twinning and softphone apps
  • Auto-attendant and call routing
  • System training and ongoing support

Data Cabling & Networks

Structured cabling, fiber installation, and network infrastructure built to handle today's bandwidth demands and tomorrow's growth — done right the first time.

  • Cat6/Cat6A and fiber optic cabling
  • Network closet organization and labeling
  • Wireless access point deployment
  • Post-installation certification testing

Surveillance & Security

IP camera systems, access control, and security infrastructure that keeps your people, property, and assets protected — with remote monitoring capabilities built in.

  • HD IP and PTZ camera systems
  • Cloud and NVR storage options
  • Access control and door hardware
  • Remote viewing via mobile app

Cloud PBX Solutions

Fully hosted cloud phone platforms that eliminate the need for on-site hardware, giving your team enterprise-grade call management from any device, anywhere.

  • No on-site server required
  • Rapid deployment for new locations
  • Unified communications integration
  • Predictable monthly pricing

Call Center Solutions

Inbound and outbound call center platforms with queue management, agent reporting, and IVR design — whether you run 5 agents or 50.

  • ACD queue management
  • Real-time and historical reporting
  • CRM integrations
  • Wallboard and supervisor dashboards

GPS Fleet Tracking

Real-time GPS fleet management that gives you full visibility into vehicle location, driver behavior, and route efficiency — reducing costs and improving accountability.

  • Live vehicle tracking dashboard
  • Geofencing and alert notifications
  • Mileage and fuel reporting
  • Driver behavior scoring
