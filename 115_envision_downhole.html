What We Do

Downhole Services Built for Real Conditions

Every service we offer has been refined over decades of actual field work — not in a boardroom, but at the wellhead where the decisions matter.

Intervention

Fishing and Well Intervention

Complex fishing operations and wellbore intervention solutions. When something is stuck, lost, or in the way of production, we have the experience and tools to solve it.

Completions

Cased Hole Completions

Bridge plug runs for isolation and remedial work. Retrievable packer systems. Cased hole tools designed for precision placement and reliable performance.

Workover

Workover Services

Full-scope wellbore workover including SWD (Saltwater Disposal) well specialization. We restore well integrity and return assets to productive capacity.

Pumping

High-Pressure Pumping

5,000 and 10,000 PSI pump capabilities for a range of wellbore applications requiring precise pressure control.

Equipment

Power Swivels and Laydown

Power swivel operations and pipe laydown unit services integrated with your tubing and rod string workover programs.

SWD Wells

SWD Well Services

Specialized workover and completion services for saltwater disposal wells, including mechanical integrity testing and remediation.

Proprietary Technology

InVision: Real-Time Field Intelligence

InVision is the enterprise resource planning and field tracking system we built specifically for the downhole and well intervention sector — because the generic off-the-shelf platforms don't understand what happens at 10,000 feet.

Developed by our own downhole specialists, InVision gives operators and clients secure, real-time access to job status, compliance documentation, and operational data from any device.

  • Real-time job tracking accessible from any device in the field
  • Secure data access with role-based permissions
  • Reduces operational risk through better information flow
  • Creates efficiency across multi-well programs
  • Delivers audit-ready documentation automatically
  • Built by downhole engineers — not generic software developers
