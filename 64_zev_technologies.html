Our Origin

Born From
Competition

ZEV Technologies started in 2006 with a single, obsessive goal: build the best Glock trigger ever made. Not a good trigger. Not a better trigger. The best one — period.

We pioneered the first drop-in replacement trigger for Glock. To this day, it remains the gold standard for competitive shooters worldwide.

What started as a trigger company grew into something more. Our engineering team expanded into slides, barrels, complete pistols, and rifle platforms — always asking the same question: can we make this better than anything else available?

The answer, consistently, has been yes. That's not arrogance. It's the only standard we accept. In 2024, ZEV was acquired by SilencerCo — a move that expands our resources while keeping the engineering-first culture intact. We're now headquartering in Riverton, Utah, where the political climate for firearms manufacturers reflects the values of the community we've always served.
