Understanding Your Options

What Is a Folding Camper?

Forget everything you think you know about pop-up campers. These aren't your parents' canvas fold-out. Aliners have hard walls, real insulation, and they set up faster than you can back into a campsite.

Canvas pop-ups leak. They grow mold. They're miserable in the rain. Aliners don't have any of those problems. Hard walls, real roof, done. Our flagship brand, Aliner, pioneered the A-frame hard-sided design that gives you the lightweight convenience of a pop-up with the durability and weather protection of a hard-wall camper.

Hard-Sided (Aliner) OUR SPECIALTY

  • Rigid aluminum/composite walls
  • Real insulation & weather protection
  • No canvas to rot, mold, or replace
  • 30-second setup time
  • Year-round camping capable

Soft-Sided (Traditional)

  • Canvas or vinyl side walls
  • Less insulation, seasonal use
  • Canvas leaks and grows mold
  • Crank-up setup takes longer
  • Miserable in the rain

Standard & Available Features

Built for Real Camping

Don't let the compact size fool you. Aliners pack a real kitchen, real sleeping, and real weather protection into something your car can tow.

A-Frame Hard Walls

Rigid aluminum and composite panels. No canvas flapping in the wind, no leaks, no mildew. When it rains at Peninsula State Park at 2am, you'll be glad you're in an Aliner.

Full Kitchenette

Cook real meals at camp with a built-in two-burner stove, sink with running water, countertop space, and available refrigerator. Everything you need for campsite cooking.

Comfortable Sleeping

Convertible dinette transforms into a spacious sleeping area with room for two adults. Some models offer additional sleeping space for up to four occupants.

Dinette Area

A proper booth-style dinette gives you a dedicated space for meals, card games, or morning coffee. Folds flat to become part of the sleeping area at night.

Climate Control Options

Available A/C and furnace so you can camp comfortably in July heat or September chill. Hard walls hold temperature in a way canvas never will — which matters a lot in Wisconsin.

Electrical Systems

12V DC and 110V AC electrical systems power interior lighting, charge devices, and run appliances. Available solar panel prep keeps you powered off-grid.
