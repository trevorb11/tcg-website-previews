What We Purchase

The Claims We Buy

Argo Partners acquires financial interests across the full spectrum of distressed situations. If you hold a claim against a distressed or insolvent entity, there is a good chance we can help you monetize it today.

01

Unsecured Creditor Claims

Trade creditors holding general unsecured claims in Chapter 7 or Chapter 11 cases can receive immediate payment rather than waiting years for a plan distribution.

02

Administrative Claims (503(b)(9))

Goods delivered to a debtor within 20 days of its bankruptcy filing may qualify for administrative priority treatment. We purchase these high-value claims swiftly.

03

Vendor & Trade Claims

Suppliers, distributors, and service providers holding outstanding invoices against a bankrupt company can convert their receivable into immediate liquidity with Argo.

04

Landlord / Lease Rejection Claims

When a debtor rejects a commercial lease, the resulting damages claim can take years to resolve. We buy these claims at fair market value without the wait.

05

Insurance Liquidation Claims

Policyholders and claimants in insurance company insolvencies face a drawn-out state liquidation process. Argo purchases these claims to provide near-term recovery.

06

Class Action Litigation Rights

Class members entitled to future settlement distributions often prefer certainty today. We evaluate and purchase litigation claims and judgment rights on a case-by-case basis.

07

Canadian CCAA / BIA Claims

Creditors in Companies' Creditors Arrangement Act and Bankruptcy and Insolvency Act proceedings in Canada can access the same immediate liquidity solutions we provide in the U.S.

08

UK Administration Claims

Creditors in UK administration and liquidation proceedings benefit from Argo's cross-border experience, allowing them to exit their position quickly rather than navigating lengthy foreign insolvency processes.

Our Story

Built on Three Decades of Distressed Debt Expertise

Argo Partners was founded in 1992 by Michael Singer as an investment fund dedicated to acquiring creditors' claims held against distressed and bankrupt companies. What started as a focused U.S. operation has grown into a firm that operates across U.S., Canadian, and UK insolvency markets.

Our core expertise remains unchanged: providing liquidity to creditors who would rather receive fair value today than endure the uncertainty of a lengthy bankruptcy process. We have facilitated hundreds of claim purchases across industries — from retail and manufacturing to financial services and real estate.

Argo does not invest in operating companies. We acquire financial interests — unsecured claims, trade receivables, litigation rights, lease rejection damages, and contract rejections — and we do so with speed, clarity, and integrity.
