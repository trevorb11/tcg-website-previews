About Brett

A Worcester Kid Who
Built Something Real

Brett Bartelson grew up right here — Flagg Street Elementary, Forest Grove Middle, Doherty Memorial High School. He earned his degree in Business Administration from Worcester State and in 2009 started VIP Landscaping with a straightforward idea: do excellent work, be honest, and take care of the people in your community. No shortcuts. No excuses.

More than fifteen years later, VIP is one of Central Massachusetts' most recognized landscaping and snow removal companies. The commercial snow contracts are the backbone of the business — Brett's clients depend on clear lots before they open their doors, and he does not miss. Residential landscaping is where the creativity shows. Both matter equally to him.

"I grew up on these streets. When I maintain your yard or plow your lot, it is not just a job — it is my neighborhood. That changes how you approach the work."

Brett is still hands-on in the business, and the team he has built reflects who he is: responsive when it counts, reliable when it is inconvenient, and genuinely proud of what they leave behind.
