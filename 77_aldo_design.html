Our Story

From Bayonne to the Northeast

In 1972, Aldo Benavides Sr. started a carpet installation business in Bayonne, New Jersey with a straightforward belief: deliver quality service at fair prices without ever compromising your integrity. He called it Aldo Carpets.

The business grew steadily, built on customer trust and a reputation for getting the job done right. In 1992, his three sons — Aldo Jr., Albert, and Alex — joined the operation and brought new energy, expanded product lines, and a broader geographic reach to what had become a well-respected regional business.

By 2006, the company's offerings had expanded so far beyond carpet that a name change made sense. Aldo Carpets became Aldo Design Group — a name that better reflected the full-service flooring and interior design resource the business had become.

Today, operating from a 30,000 square foot design center and warehouse in Carteret, NJ, Aldo Design Group serves homeowners, commercial clients, and builders across the entire Northeast — with the same family values that started it all over 50 years ago.
