Our Story

A Table Set for Everyone

Nanna's Place started with a simple idea: food made the way it used to be, from scratch, with care, and served by people who are genuinely glad to see you. Linda Roth opened these doors because she believed Gresham needed a spot where strangers become regulars and regulars become family.

Everything we serve is made in-house. Our biscuits come out of a real oven. Our eggs are cracked fresh every morning. Our soups are made from scratch. We don't cut corners because Nanna never did, and we're not about to start now.

Whether you're grabbing breakfast before work, catching up with a friend over the Reuben, or settling in for a Thursday night Chicken Fried Steak, there's always a seat here with your name on it. Come as you are.

— Linda Roth, Owner

More Than a Meal

A Place to Slow Down

Nanna's Place was never just about the food. It's about having a real reason to sit down, look up from your phone, and enjoy the people across the table. We have room for big families, tables for two, and plenty of patio space for the kids to run while the grown-ups have a second cup of coffee.

The outdoor patio is covered and heated year-round, which means even on a gray Gresham morning you can sit outside with your dog and not regret it. The yard games are out there too, because why shouldn't lunch come with a little friendly competition?

Rooted in Gresham

Part of the Neighborhood

Nanna's Place is a Gresham restaurant through and through. We're not a chain, we're not a formula, and we're not run from a spreadsheet somewhere else. We're your neighbors, and we take that seriously.

We're a proud member of the Gresham Area Chamber of Commerce because we believe in this city. When Gresham does well, we do well. That's exactly how it should work.

Every time you choose to eat here instead of somewhere else, you're keeping this corner of Main Ave exactly what it should be: local, real, and worth coming back to. Thank you for that.

Gresham Area Chamber Member
