Our Story

130 Years of Connecticut Craftsmanship

A. Secondino & Son was founded in 1895 — before most of today's largest construction firms existed. What started as a local contracting operation in coastal Connecticut has grown across five generations without ever losing what made it: personal accountability, pride in the work, and the belief that a client's trust is earned on the job site, not in a sales meeting.

Alfred Secondino leads the company today with the same standards his family established more than a century ago. A. Secondino & Son is prequalified with the Connecticut DOT and maintains active relationships with municipalities and institutions across the state — the kind of relationships that are only possible when you deliver, project after project, year after year.
