Who We Are

A Global Aviation Consulting Firm with Local Commitment

Founded in 2015 in Summit, New Jersey, Merchant Aviation was built to bring a new level of strategic thinking to airport development. We're not an architecture firm that learned aviation on the job, and we're not an engineering company trying to do consulting. We're aviation consultants — through and through.

Our team brings together expertise in airport planning, financial feasibility, capital program management, and passenger experience — the full spectrum of what it takes to develop an airport that works for the communities it serves and the airlines that operate in it.

In recent years, Merchant Aviation has become part of the Groupe ADP family — one of the world's leading airport operators, managing 29 airports worldwide. That partnership gives our clients access to global knowledge and networks while maintaining the responsive, results-oriented service that defined us from the beginning.

What We Do

Full-Spectrum Airport Consulting Services

From the first feasibility study to the ribbon-cutting ceremony, we guide airport development projects through every phase with strategic clarity and operational precision.

Strategic Visioning and Master Planning

Long-range airport master plans that align with community needs, aviation demand forecasts, and long-term financial sustainability.

Airport Planning and Programming

Facility programming, capacity analysis, and concept-level planning that sets the foundation for every successful airport project.

Capital Improvement Program Management

End-to-end management of multi-million dollar airport capital programs — on time, on budget, and compliant with FAA requirements.

Financial Feasibility Studies

Rigorous financial analysis and feasibility studies to evaluate project viability, funding strategies, and return on investment.

Architectural Design Coordination

Design coordination and quality oversight across terminal, landside, and airside infrastructure projects.

Construction Management

Owner-representative construction management services ensuring projects are delivered per specifications, on schedule, and within budget.

Passenger Experience Consulting

Passenger flow analysis, terminal design optimization, and amenity planning that creates airports people actually enjoy using.

Operational Efficiency Analysis

Process and operations assessment to identify inefficiencies and recommend improvements that enhance airport performance and profitability.
