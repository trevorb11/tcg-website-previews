What We Offer

Comprehensive Tax & Financial Services

From personal returns to complex business financials, we provide the full range of services Lancaster families and businesses need to succeed.

Individual Tax Returns

Accurate, thorough preparation of federal and state personal income tax returns, maximizing your refund and minimizing your liability.

Business Tax Returns

Expert preparation for sole proprietors, LLCs, S-Corps, C-Corps, and partnerships — ensuring full compliance and smart deductions.

Tax Planning

Proactive strategies to reduce your tax burden year-round — not just at filing time. We help individuals and businesses plan ahead with confidence.

Payroll Services

Reliable payroll processing, direct deposit, quarterly filings, and year-end W-2/1099 preparation so you can focus on running your business.

Bookkeeping

Organized, accurate bookkeeping services that keep your financials in order — monthly reconciliations, financial statements, and clean records year-round.

Estate, Trust & CFO Services

Specialized preparation for estate and trust tax returns, plus strategic CFO/advisory services to guide your business's financial decision-making.

About Us

Led by a Trusted Local Expert

Bryan Struckmann — IRS Registered Tax Preparer

Since founding Advantage Tax & Business Services in 2013, Bryan Struckmann has built a reputation in Lancaster County for delivering honest, accurate, and personalized financial services that truly make a difference for clients.

Bryan holds IRS registration as an authorized tax preparer, bringing professional accountability and a commitment to continuing education to every client relationship. Whether you're a first-time filer or a growing business, Bryan takes the time to understand your complete financial picture.

Located in the heart of Lancaster at 2137 Embassy Drive, Advantage Tax is a community-focused firm where you're always a priority — never just a number in a queue.
