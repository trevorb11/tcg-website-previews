Est. 1970

The Heeringa Family

55 years of concrete. The same family. The same standard.

Bode's Precast started manufacturing in Lynden, Washington in 1970. Rick Heeringa grew up in the concrete business — ready-mix first, then precast — and has been at it for over 40 years. In 2010 he moved the operation to Everson, where every product is still made by hand by a team that cares about what it is building. When you call Bode's, you are likely talking to Rick. He knows the products inside out and will tell you straight what you need for the job.
