About the Firm

Forty Years of Fighting for the Injured

Silver & Kelmachter, LLP is a personal injury and medical malpractice firm that has served injured New Yorkers from our Manhattan office for decades. We are not a generalist firm that handles everything. We focus exclusively on the cases that matter most — when someone has been seriously hurt and deserves to know that a skilled, experienced attorney is fighting in their corner.

Every client we represent receives direct, personal attention from our attorneys. We investigate thoroughly, retain the best expert witnesses available, and pursue maximum recovery — whether through negotiation or, when necessary, trial.

Our representation is on a contingency fee basis. That means you pay nothing unless we win. We take that responsibility seriously, and we accept only cases we genuinely believe in.

Practice Areas

Cases We Handle

We focus on serious, complex injury cases where skilled legal representation makes the difference between an inadequate settlement and the recovery our clients deserve.

Personal Injury

Car accidents, slip and fall, premises liability, construction site accidents, and all manner of injuries caused by the negligence of another. We investigate fully and fight for maximum compensation.

Medical Malpractice

When a healthcare provider's negligence causes harm — surgical errors, misdiagnosis, birth injuries, anesthesia mistakes, medication errors — we hold them accountable with rigorous medical and legal expertise.

Catastrophic Injuries

Traumatic brain injuries, spinal cord injuries, severe burns, amputations, and wrongful death. These cases demand attorneys with the experience and resources to pursue the full value of a life profoundly changed.

Childhood Vaccine Injury

Cases before the National Vaccine Injury Compensation Program in the U.S. Court of Federal Claims. Perry Silver has obtained one of the top awards nationally in this highly specialized area of federal law.

Product Liability

Defective products that cause serious harm — from medical devices to consumer products to industrial equipment. Manufacturer and distributor accountability for the injuries their products cause.

Premises Liability

Property owners have a duty to maintain safe conditions. When they fail and someone is injured, we build the strongest possible case — from evidence preservation through expert testimony and trial if needed.

Meet Perry D. Silver

Partner | Personal Injury & Medical Malpractice

Perry Silver has spent his career doing one thing: fighting for people who have been seriously hurt. He graduated from the State University of New York, Buffalo School of Law in 1979 and has been dedicated to personal injury and medical malpractice ever since — because he understood, early on, that injured people need advocates with real expertise and genuine commitment.

Over more than four decades of practice, Perry has obtained landmark results for his clients. His work in the National Vaccine Injury Compensation Program resulted in what was then one of the three largest awards nationally — a case requiring mastery of federal court procedure, complex medical evidence, and the kind of tenacious advocacy that defines his practice.

He is a member of the American Association for Justice, selected to Super Lawyers, and admitted to practice in all four federal district courts in New York as well as the United States Court of Federal Claims — credentials that reflect a career built on doing the hard work of serious injury litigation.
