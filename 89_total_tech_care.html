Our Story

Built in South Florida. Built for South Florida.

Total Technical Care has been a trusted IT partner for South Florida businesses since 2001. We were founded on the belief that small and medium businesses deserve access to the same quality of IT support and infrastructure that large enterprises take for granted — without the enterprise price tag.

Our clients are the businesses that make South Florida run: professional service firms, healthcare practices, legal offices, retail businesses, and non-profits who depend on their technology to serve their customers and clients every day. When their technology works, their business works.
