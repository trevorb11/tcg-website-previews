What We Do

Commercial Roofing Services

From new construction installs to the leak three other contractors gave up on — Alper Enterprises handles the full spectrum of commercial, industrial, and institutional roofing across South Jersey and the greater Philadelphia area. The unglamorous work that keeps buildings functional. We own it.

01

Built-Up Roofing

Multi-layer BUR systems engineered for long-term performance on flat and low-slope commercial structures. Proven technology, expertly installed.

02

Modified Bitumen

Torch-applied and cold-applied modified bitumen membranes for superior flexibility and durability in the Mid-Atlantic climate.

03

EPDM & TPO Systems

Single-ply membrane systems that deliver energy efficiency and weather resistance — installed and seamed to manufacturer specifications.

04

Sheet Metal & Flashing

Precision fabricated sheet metal work including copings, gutters, downspouts, counterflashing, and custom architectural details.

05

Historical Restoration

Sensitive, code-compliant roofing work on historic structures — preserving architectural character while ensuring modern performance standards.

06

Leak Investigation & Repair

We find and fix the leaks that others can't. Forensic investigation, targeted repairs, and permanent solutions — not temporary patches.

07

New Roof Installation

Complete tear-off and replacement programs for commercial buildings of all types — designed to maximize useful life and minimize disruption.

08

Preventive Maintenance

Scheduled inspection and maintenance programs that extend roof life, catch problems early, and protect your capital investment year-round.

Our Story

Built on a Journeyman's Standards

Craig Alper learned roofing from the top of a building, not the top of an org chart. He came up through the trade — journeyman, foreman, project manager — until he was running one of the largest commercial roofing operations in the country. Then he founded Alper Enterprises in Moorestown, NJ in 1991, because he'd seen what commercial roofing looked like when it was done the right way, and he wanted to build a company that actually did it.

More than thirty years later, Alper Enterprises is still serving South Jersey, Philadelphia, and the surrounding region. The crew is smaller by design. The attention to each job is not.

"A roof is not glamorous work. But when it fails, everything inside fails with it. That responsibility is why I still walk every job myself."

— Craig Alper, Owner

Craig is still on job sites. His senior tradespeople are still involved from estimate through final walkthrough. That's not something Alper markets — it's just how the company works.
