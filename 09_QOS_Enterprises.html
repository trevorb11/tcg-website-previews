Masking & Selective Plating

Finishing & Surface Prep

Chemical conversion coating (chromate), passivation of stainless steel, phosphating, and surface preparation for adhesive bonding. Improves corrosion resistance and coating adhesion to specification.

ISO 9000 Is Not a Plaque on Our Wall.
It Is How We Work Every Day.

Our ISO 9000 certified quality management system was built by people who understand that a plating defect on a defense component is not a warranty issue — it is a mission risk. Every part that leaves our Rochester facility has been inspected for coating thickness, adhesion, and dimensional conformance. Every order ships with full documentation.

From First Article Inspection through high-volume production runs, we provide the traceability records and certificates of conformance that federal and prime contractors require without having to ask for them twice.

  • Coating thickness verification on every order
  • Certificate of Conformance (C of C) provided with shipment
  • First Article Inspection (FAI) support and full plating reports
  • Material traceability from raw chemical to finished part
  • In-process and final inspection per applicable specification
