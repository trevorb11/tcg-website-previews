Our Approach

The Design of
Difficult Moments.

Since the early 1990s, we've asked a question that most architects don't spend much time with: what does it feel like to be in this space when you're frightened, in pain, or sitting beside someone you love who is sick?

Werner Buckl founded this firm in 1971. In 2006, four of its employees purchased it and formed Buckl Architects, Inc. — a transition that wasn't about exit or acquisition, but about stewardship. The people who own it now are the ones who show up to your project every day.

The Patient Experience
Every material choice, every corridor width, every window placement is filtered through a single question: how does this feel to someone who is frightened, in pain, or uncertain? Comfort is not an aesthetic luxury in a healthcare setting — it is a clinical tool that affects outcomes.
2
55 Years of Institutional Knowledge
Founded in 1971, Buckl has watched healthcare design evolve through multiple eras — and shaped it on projects at Morristown Medical Center, Overlook, Thomas Jefferson, and others. That institutional memory is not decorative. It informs every decision.
3
Employee Ownership Since 2006
When four employees purchased the firm in 2006, something important shifted — the people signing off on your project's details are the same people whose names are on the ownership papers. That accountability runs through everything.

Regional Coverage, Local Accountability
