About Morning Star Tours

Thirty Years of Walking Groups Through Scripture, One Country at a Time

In 1995, Cheri Fitzsimmons returned from her first trip to Israel and could not stop talking about it. The way the landscape made the Gospels come alive. The way standing in Gethsemane at night shifted something she could not quite name. She and her husband Mike founded Morning Star Tours because they believed every believer deserved to have that experience.

Thirty years later, Cheri is still planning the itineraries, still traveling with groups, still moved by the moment when a first-time pilgrim rounds a corner in Jerusalem and the text they have read a hundred times suddenly becomes real. That has never gotten old.

"I have watched people weep in Gethsemane, laugh with new friends on the Sea of Galilee, and come home as different versions of themselves. That is not me. That is what happens when you meet the Word in the land where it was written."

Cheri Fitzsimmons — Founder, Morning Star Tours — Since 1995
