Asset Classes

We Trade Across Every Major Category of Receivable

Debt Market handles charged-off portfolios across consumer and commercial asset types, with specialized expertise in high-volume categories and emerging asset classes.

Compliance and Security

Regulatory Rigor at Every Step

The debt purchasing industry is heavily regulated, and for good reason. Consumer information, chain-of-title integrity, and collection practice standards are areas where failure creates significant legal and reputational risk.

Debt Market has built compliance into every layer of how we operate — not as a checkbox exercise, but as the foundation of how we protect buyers, sellers, and the consumers whose data flows through every transaction.

We maintain nationwide licensing, align with RMAI member standards, enforce strict data security protocols on all portfolio data rooms, and provide complete chain-of-title documentation on every transaction we facilitate.
