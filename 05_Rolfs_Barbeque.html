The Pitmaster

A Doctor Who Cooks
Because He Loves It.

Dr. Rolf Hissom has spent decades caring for patients in Muskegon. The kind of doctor who remembers your name and your family. On his days off, he disappears behind a smoker.

The barbeque started the way all great things do — feeding people he cared about. Weekend cookouts for family. Neighbors showing up uninvited with empty plates. Word spread the way it does in a town like Muskegon. Eventually the question stopped being whether to open a restaurant and started being when.

The same precision and care that makes Dr. Rolf a great physician is what makes his barbeque different. Low and slow, hardwood only, no shortcuts, no compromise. When the brisket isn't right, it doesn't go out. Period. Muskegon has been eating well ever since.
