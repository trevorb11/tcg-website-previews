How We Work

Straight Talk. No Surprises.

Our process is built around honest communication, realistic timelines, and craftsmanship that you can see and touch when we're done.

01

Free Consultation

Javier visits your site, listens to your vision, and assesses the scope. No assumptions, no generic quotes — we want to see your project before we tell you what it costs.

02

Detailed Estimate

A transparent, itemized proposal with clear scope of work, material specifications, timeline, and pricing. We explain every line so there are no surprises down the road.

03

Our licensed crews get to work. Javier maintains hands-on involvement and keeps you updated at every major milestone — with weekly check-ins and direct access by phone.

About Javier Enciso

Built by Someone Who Knows What It Takes

Javier Enciso founded Enco Operations from Chicago's Southwest Side with a straightforward commitment: do honest work, charge a fair price, and never cut corners on the things that matter. That's the business he's built, and it's the one he shows up to run every single day.

Javier is not a contractor who wins the job and hands it to a crew. He's on the site. He takes the calls. He walks the punch list. When you hire Enco Operations, you get Javier's personal accountability attached to every project — and that's something no amount of marketing can replicate.

Over more than eight years building in Chicago, Enco Operations has compiled a track record that includes 24+ permitted projects totaling more than $49 million in project value. That record was built one project at a time, by doing the work right and earning repeat business from clients who trusted Javier with their homes, their investments, and their communities.
