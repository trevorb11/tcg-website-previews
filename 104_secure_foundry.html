// THE CHALLENGE

A Critical Gap in the U.S. Supply Chain

The United States has become dangerously dependent on overseas semiconductor manufacturing for defense-critical components. When overseas fabs face disruption — geopolitical, economic, or otherwise — programs that depend on them face delays, cost overruns, and in some cases, unacceptable security risks.

The problem is especially acute for low-volume and mixed-volume requirements. Large overseas foundries optimize for high-volume consumer chip production. Defense programs, research institutions, and technology startups that need small-batch production, obsolete part reproduction, or custom ASIC fabrication often find themselves with nowhere to turn domestically.

SecureFoundry was founded specifically to fill that gap — a domestic foundry purpose-built for the needs of defense, academia, and frontier technology applications where security, flexibility, and domestic production are not optional.

// HBA TECHNOLOGY

The Hyper-Beam Array Advantage

At the core of SecureFoundry's capabilities is our breakthrough Hyper-Beam Array (HBA) Lithography System — a maskless patterning platform that eliminates the need for traditional photomasks entirely.

Traditional chip fabrication requires expensive photomasks for every new design iteration. A single mask set can cost hundreds of thousands of dollars and takes weeks to produce. For defense programs and startups that need rapid design iteration, this is prohibitive.

HBA changes that equation. Because our system patterns directly on the wafer without a mask, we can accommodate new designs in days rather than weeks, at costs that make small-batch and prototype production genuinely accessible. The result is faster time-to-silicon, lower iteration costs, and greater flexibility for programs that cannot wait.

Foundry Services

SecureFoundry offers a comprehensive suite of manufacturing and commercialization services for defense, academic, and commercial clients.

Low-Volume Chip Manufacturing

Short-run and mixed-volume chip production at scales that commercial fabs will not accommodate — from prototype quantities through multi-wafer production runs for defense and specialty applications.

Reverse Engineering

Reproduction of legacy and obsolete components for defense programs that cannot migrate to newer parts. We re-create the chip, validate its functionality, and deliver a domestically sourced replacement.

Cost-Effective Prototyping

Rapid design-to-silicon prototyping without the photomask cost. Iterate on your design quickly, validate functionality, and move to production on a timeline that defense programs require.

Legacy Part Reproduction

When critical components go end-of-life, we source and reproduce them domestically. Our legacy production capability keeps fielded systems operational without depending on foreign supply chains.

Commercialization Platform

For academic innovations and startup IP, our commercialization platform handles IP consolidation, design management, risk analysis, supply chain tracking, and production launch — from lab to market.

Secure Facility Production

All production occurs in ITAR-controlled, NIST 800-171-compliant secure facilities with the physical and procedural security infrastructure that classified and sensitive programs require.

// LEADERSHIP

Built From the Inside of National Security

Lex Keen
// FOUNDER | FORMER TECHNICAL DIRECTOR, U.S. CYBER COMMAND

SecureFoundry was not founded by a semiconductor industry veteran looking to carve out a niche. It was founded by Lex Keen, a former Technical Director at U.S. Cyber Command who experienced firsthand the vulnerability of American defense technology to foreign supply chain disruption.

Lex founded SecureFoundry in 2016 with a mission statement that has not changed: democratize access to critical semiconductor manufacturing technology while ensuring that America's most sensitive applications never have to depend on overseas fabrication. The Hyper-Beam Array was developed specifically in service of that mission — eliminating the photomask barrier that makes domestic low-volume production economically prohibitive.

Today, SecureFoundry operates from Fort Worth, Texas with additional facilities in Maryland and the Netherlands — providing trusted domestic production with the geographic coverage that global defense and commercial clients require.
