Our Story

A Family Business Built on Results and Relationships

Empire Building Services started as a family operation in Santa Ana in 1982, when Suzanne and John DeRossett saw an opportunity to build a company that did two things most contractors struggle with: quality work and honest communication.

More than four decades later, we've grown into one of Southern California's most respected commercial service contractors — serving property managers, healthcare facilities, office parks, and retail centers across Los Angeles and Orange County.

We're still a family business. Suzanne still leads the company with the same hands-on approach that built our reputation. When you work with Empire, you get people who take personal pride in every space we touch.

Consultation and Site Walk
