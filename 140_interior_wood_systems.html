What We Build

Complete Custom Woodworking
Under One Roof

Every project — from initial design to final installation — is handled by our skilled craftsmen in our Rohnert Park shop. No subcontracting. No surprises.

Custom Cabinet Manufacturing

Designed and built to your exact specifications in our Rohnert Park shop. From face-frame to frameless, painted to natural wood — every cabinet is a piece of furniture.

Cabinet Installation

Expert installation ensures perfect level, plumb, and fit. Our installers are the same craftsmen who built your cabinets — so they know every detail of every piece.

Millwork

Crown moldings, wainscoting, ceiling beams, built-in shelving, and architectural details that transform a house into a home with lasting character and depth.

Finish Carpentry

The details that define a room. Baseboards, casing, chair rails, and custom built-ins installed with the precision and care that separate good work from exceptional work.

Door Installation

Interior and exterior door hanging done right the first time. Proper fitting, weather sealing, and hardware alignment for doors that operate smoothly for decades.

Custom Trim

Window casings, coffered ceilings, decorative columns, and one-of-a-kind trim profiles. If you can envision it, we can mill it and install it to perfection.

Premium Materials

Wood That Ages
Into an Heirloom

We work with the finest domestic and imported hardwoods, selected by hand for each project. Your choice of species defines the soul of the piece.

White Oak
Timeless & Versatile
Open, cathedral grain with warm, honeyed tones. Takes stain beautifully and ages to a rich patina. The choice for transitional and Craftsman styles.
Black Walnut
Rich & Dramatic
Deep chocolate tones with swirling figure. North America's most prized cabinet wood — extraordinary as a statement piece or island top.
Hard Maple
Clean & Contemporary
Tight, consistent grain with a creamy white face. The gold standard for painted cabinetry — holds finish flawlessly and machines to perfect edges.
Cherry
Warm & Refined
Begins a blush pink and deepens to a rich auburn over years of light exposure. A living finish that grows more beautiful with time.

About Us

Built on Craft.
Driven by Pride.

Interior Wood Systems, Inc. is a Rohnert Park-based custom cabinet and millwork company serving the entire Sonoma County and North Bay region. Founded by master craftsman Dave Ghiglieri, the company was built on a simple principle: every piece of woodwork we touch should look and feel like fine furniture.

From our 137-B Utility Court workshop, we design, mill, and build everything in-house — giving us complete control over quality at every stage. No middlemen. No outsourced production. Just skilled hands and an unwavering commitment to the craft.
