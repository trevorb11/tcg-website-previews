What We Haul

Freight Services

From full truckloads to specialized cargo, Night Trucking delivers with the care and precision your freight demands — every run, every mile.

Full Truckload (FTL)

Dedicated trailer capacity for your full shipment. Faster transit, fewer touches, and maximum freight security from origin to destination.

Less Than Truckload (LTL)

Cost-effective shipping for smaller loads. We consolidate your freight with other shipments to save you money without sacrificing reliability.

Dry Van

Enclosed trailers protecting your general freight from the elements. Perfect for palletized goods, packaged products, and non-perishable cargo.

Flatbed

Open trailer hauling for oversized, heavy, or unusually shaped freight — machinery, building materials, steel, and equipment that won't fit enclosed.

Refrigerated

Temperature-controlled transport for perishables, pharmaceuticals, and food-grade products. Maintaining cold chain integrity from pickup to delivery.

Expedited Freight

When time is critical, we move. Dedicated runs and priority scheduling ensure your urgent shipments arrive on-time, every time.

Our Story

Idaho Roots.
American Work Ethic.

Night Trucking LLC was founded in 2000 in the quiet ranching community of Malad City, Idaho. What started as a single operator's commitment to dependable freight has grown into a trusted carrier serving shippers across the Western United States.

We operate under USDOT Number 904559 and MC-394124, fully authorized for interstate property transport. As an owner-operated business, every shipment carries our personal reputation. When you work with Night Trucking, you deal directly with the people who haul your freight.
