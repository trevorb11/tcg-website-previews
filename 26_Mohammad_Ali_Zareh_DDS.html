Our Services

Everything your family needs, at one practice

Whether it is your child's first cleaning, a crown you have been putting off, or implants to replace a tooth for good — we do it all. No referrals to juggle, no starting over with a new office.

⚪

Preventive Care

Regular cleanings and exams are the foundation of a healthy mouth. We take the time to review your x-rays with you and answer any questions honestly.

🔣

Restorative Dentistry

Fillings, crowns, bridges, and root canals to restore your teeth to full function and natural appearance.

🦷

Dental Implants

Permanent tooth replacement solutions that look, feel, and function just like your natural teeth.

😌

Cosmetic Dentistry

Teeth whitening, veneers, and smile makeovers to give you the confidence that comes with a beautiful smile.

👤

Pediatric Dentistry

Kids who feel safe at the dentist grow into adults who actually go. Our team is patient, kind, and good at making little ones feel like they are in good hands.

🛈

Emergency Dentistry

Dental pain does not keep office hours. When something goes wrong, call us first. We do our best to get you seen the same day.

Meet Your Doctor

Dr. Mohammad Ali Zareh, DDS

Dr. Zareh grew up in the Inland Empire and earned his undergraduate degree right here at UC Riverside before completing his Doctor of Dental Surgery at the University of Southern California. He opened the first Dental Views location in 2010 with a straightforward goal: build a practice where patients genuinely feel cared for. Sixteen years and 12 locations later, a 4.88-star rating across thousands of reviews says more than any credential could.
