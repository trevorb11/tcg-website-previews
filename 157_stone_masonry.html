About Us

A Legacy Built
Stone by Stone

Stone & Masonry Inc. is a third-generation family business rooted in Santa Barbara, CA. Founded on a passion for natural materials and enduring craftsmanship, we have spent decades honing the art of stone and masonry work across the Central Coast.

Led by owner Ramon Lazcano, our team brings together traditional masonry knowledge and modern construction expertise to deliver work that exceeds expectations on every job — from intimate residential patios to large-scale commercial installations.

  • Locally owned and operated in Santa Barbara since the 1990s
  • California-licensed Concrete & Masonry contractor (Lic. #715033)
  • Serving Montecito, Goleta, Carpinteria, Summerland, and the greater Santa Barbara area
  • BuildZoom score of 95 — top 22% of California licensed contractors
