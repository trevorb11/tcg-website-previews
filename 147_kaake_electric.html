What We Do

Electrical Services for
Home & Business

From routine installations to complex panel work, Kaake Electric handles every job with precision and care.

🔌
Panel Upgrades

Modernize your electrical panel to handle today's power demands safely. We upgrade breaker boxes and service entrances for homes and commercial spaces.

🔧
Wiring & Rewiring

New construction wiring and full rewiring of older homes. We bring outdated wiring up to code and handle additions, renovations, and more.

💡
Lighting Installation

Recessed lighting, LED retrofits, outdoor lighting, landscape illumination, and smart lighting systems installed cleanly and efficiently.

🚗
EV Charger Installation

Level 2 home EV charging station installation for all vehicle makes. We handle permitting, panel assessment, and a clean, professional install.

⚡
Generator Installation

Whole-home standby generators and portable generator hookups. Stay powered during New England's nor'easters and storm outages.

🌀
Ceiling Fan Installation

New ceiling fan installations and replacements, including junction box upgrades when needed for safe, code-compliant mounting.

🔵
Outlet & Switch Installation

GFCI and AFCI outlet installation, USB outlets, whole-house surge protection, dimmer switches, and smart switch upgrades.

📋
Code Compliance & Inspections

Electrical inspections for real estate transactions, rental properties, and code compliance work to meet current Massachusetts electrical standards.

🚨
Emergency Electrical Service

Power outages, tripped breakers, sparking outlets, and burning smells can't wait. We offer around-the-clock emergency response across the South Shore.
