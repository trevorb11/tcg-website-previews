About Us

If It Floats Through Florida, We Can Supply It.

OMS International was built around one simple idea: when a ship is in port, it needs supplies, coordination, and logistics handled by people who already know the dock — not someone learning on the fly.

We're based in Miami's maritime corridor, which puts us close to PortMiami and Port Everglades — two of the busiest ports in the Western Hemisphere. Our team knows the schedules, the vendors, the regulatory environment, and how to move fast when a vessel's turnaround window is tight.

As an FMCSA-certified carrier and broker, we also handle the inland transportation piece — so a single call to our team covers provisioning, cargo coordination, and ground logistics. That's one partner, not four.

C. Ruiz and the OMS team have been doing this since 2017. If you're operating in Florida's ports, give us a call at (786) 828-3832 — we'll get you what you need.
