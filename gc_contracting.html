Meet Claude Taylor & GC Contracting

When Claude Taylor founded GC Contracting LLC in 2002, he had a simple promise: treat every home like your own, and never cut corners. That commitment has guided every project for over 20 years, earning the trust of homeowners across Prince George's County, Charles County, Howard County, and the greater Maryland area.

What started as one man's dedication to honest, quality craftsmanship has grown into a full-service contracting company. From kitchen and bathroom remodels to basement finishing, attic conversions, roofing, and waterproofing, Claude and his team bring the same hands-on attention to detail to every job, big or small.

Two decades in business means Claude has seen it all and knows how to get it done right. That experience is something no shortcut can replace, and it is the foundation of everything GC Contracting delivers.
