About GC Contracting LLC

Founded by Claude Taylor, GC Contracting LLC has been a trusted name in Maryland home remodeling for over 20 years. Since 2002, we have helped homeowners across Prince George's County, Charles County, Howard County, and the greater Maryland area transform their homes with expert craftsmanship and dependable service.

What started as a commitment to delivering honest, high-quality work has grown into a full-service contracting company offering everything from kitchen and bathroom remodels to basement finishing, attic conversions, roofing, and waterproofing. Every project we take on receives the same level of care and attention to detail.

We believe that great results come from clear communication, skilled workmanship, and treating every home as if it were our own. That is the standard Claude and the GC Contracting team bring to every job.
