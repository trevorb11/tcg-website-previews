Our Story

Led by Those Who Know the Mission

RLM Communications was founded by Randy L. Moore, a military retiree who brings decades of hands-on experience in special operations communications and conventional IT systems to every engagement. He built RLM Communications with a clear purpose: to provide the U.S. Government and Department of Defense with the kind of mission-focused, technically rigorous support that only someone who has lived the mission can deliver.

Headquartered adjacent to Fort Liberty, NC, our team consists of seasoned professionals holding Security+, Network+, and CISSP certifications — many with operational and cleared backgrounds that inform every solution we design and deploy.

As a certified Service-Disabled Veteran Owned Small Business, we are eligible for SDVOSB set-aside contracts and bring the agility of a focused team with the depth of enterprise-level expertise.
