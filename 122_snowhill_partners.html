Who We Are

An Independent Boutique Advisory Built for Those Who Value Candor

Snowhill Partners is an independent investment and strategic advisory firm serving founder-led businesses, private equity-backed companies, and family offices in the greater Philadelphia area and beyond.

Founded by James — a seasoned advisor with deep experience in capital markets, M&A transactions, and business strategy — Snowhill Partners operates on a simple principle: our clients deserve genuinely independent counsel, not advice shaped by conflicts of interest or institutional mandates.

We are boutique by design. Every engagement receives senior-level attention. Every recommendation is made with your interests, your timeline, and your values as the primary guide. Whether you're evaluating a sale, considering a capital raise, or navigating a period of significant strategic change, we provide the perspective and the relationships to help you make the right decisions.

The best advisory relationships are built on trust earned over time — not on a transaction completed last quarter.

Our Approach

How We Work With Clients

We don't follow a rigid process. But there is a framework that guides how we engage with every client — beginning with listening.

I

Confidential Discovery Meeting

A candid, no-obligation conversation to understand your situation, objectives, and timeline. All discussions are strictly confidential.

II

Situation Assessment

An honest assessment of your current position, the strategic landscape, and the options realistically available to you.

III

Strategic Options Review

We develop and present a clear-eyed review of your strategic options, with the trade-offs and implications of each spelled out plainly.

IV

Execution Planning

Once a path is chosen, we develop a detailed execution plan and begin the introductions and process steps required to execute it.

V

Transaction Support and Close

We stay with you through closing — advising, problem-solving, and advocating for your interests until the transaction is complete.
