Our Founding

Built on a Simple Principle: Objectivity Changes Everything

In 1988, Patricia Fisher founded JANUS Associates on a straightforward belief: organizations deserve security advice that serves their interests, not a vendor's bottom line. In an industry crowded with product-pushing consultancies, JANUS would be different — vendor-neutral, senior-led, and focused exclusively on the client's security outcomes.

"We don't sell hardware. We don't sell software. We sell expertise — and we stand behind every recommendation we make."

Over 37 years and more than 2,200 client engagements later, that founding principle remains the center of everything JANUS does. The firm has never had a sales quota. Every technologist maintains a minimum of eight years of experience. And every engagement is staffed by people who genuinely understand the threat landscape their clients face.

Today, JANUS serves organizations across every sector — from Fortune 500 corporations to federal agencies to nonprofit institutions — with the same commitment to objective, rigorous security counsel that Patricia Fisher instilled from day one.

What We Do

Comprehensive Security. One Trusted Advisor.

JANUS provides a full spectrum of cybersecurity and risk management services — always tailored to your specific environment, regulatory obligations, and organizational risk tolerance.

01

Vulnerability Risk Assessments

Systematic evaluation of your technical environment, processes, and controls to identify and prioritize exposures before they become incidents.

02

Penetration Testing

Authorized adversarial testing of your networks, applications, and physical controls — conducted by experienced professionals who think like attackers.

03

vCISO Services

Senior security leadership on demand. Our virtual CISO service brings executive-level strategy and accountability without the cost of a full-time hire.

04

Enterprise Risk Management

Structured frameworks for identifying, measuring, and managing organizational risk — aligned with your industry, regulatory environment, and business objectives.

05

Security Architecture

Design and review of security controls, network segmentation, identity management, and zero-trust architecture for modern enterprise environments.

06

Business Resilience

Business continuity planning, disaster recovery design, and incident response readiness — because security means keeping you operational when threats materialize.

07

Digital Forensics and eDiscovery

Expert investigation of security incidents, litigation support, and electronic evidence collection with documented chain-of-custody and legal defensibility.

08

Data Breach Crisis Response

When a breach occurs, response time is everything. Our crisis team provides rapid containment, analysis, notification support, and recovery coordination.

09

Security Awareness Training

Customized education programs that actually change behavior — because the most sophisticated technical controls fail if people aren't prepared to recognize threats.

10

Regulatory Compliance Consulting

Expert guidance through HIPAA, PCI-DSS, CMMC, FISMA, and other frameworks — translating regulatory requirements into practical, auditable security programs.

Firm Leadership

Patricia Fisher

Chief Executive Officer and President • Founder, 1988

Patricia Fisher built JANUS Associates from the ground up with a conviction that the cybersecurity industry needed at least one firm whose only loyalty was to the client. Over 37 years, she has guided the organization through every evolution of the threat landscape — from mainframe security in the late 1980s to the AI-enabled adversaries of today — without ever losing sight of that founding conviction.
