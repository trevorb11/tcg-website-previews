About Geoneering

Founded on Expertise. Built on Integrity.

Geoneering was established in 1996 by Dr. John Bryant, a geotechnical engineer whose career bridged academic rigor and real-world practice. From its roots in Carrollton, Texas, the firm has grown to serve clients across the country from offices in the Dallas-Fort Worth metroplex and Denver, Colorado.

Our work spans the full depth of the built environment — from subsurface investigations that inform foundation design to forensic analyses that untangle the root causes of structural failures. We bring the same methodical, evidence-based approach to every project, whether it is a routine geotechnical report or a high-stakes expert witness engagement.

"Sound engineering is fundamentally about understanding the ground beneath you before you build on it — and knowing how to interpret what went wrong when things don't go as planned."

Dr. John Bryant, P.E. — Founder
  • Licensed Professional Engineers in multiple states
  • Accredited geotechnical laboratory for soil and materials testing
  • LiDAR, GIS, and advanced field instrumentation capabilities
  • Extensive expert witness and litigation support experience

Technical Capabilities

Depth of Technical Knowledge

Subsurface Investigation

Geotechnical borings, SPT, cone penetration testing, Shelby tube sampling, and rock coring across a wide range of soil and site conditions.

Laboratory Testing

In-house soil classification, consolidation, shear strength, compaction, permeability, and concrete/masonry materials testing.

LiDAR & GIS Analysis

High-resolution aerial and terrestrial LiDAR data acquisition and GIS mapping for site characterization, topographic analysis, and damage documentation.

Foundation Systems

Shallow, deep, and specialty foundation analysis for residential, commercial, and infrastructure applications in expansive clay and other challenging soil environments.

Retaining Structures

Design and failure investigation of retaining walls, MSE walls, sheet pile systems, and earth retention for cuts and fills on constrained urban sites.

Drainage & Hydrology

Surface drainage assessment, stormwater analysis, infiltration testing, and flood plain evaluation in support of site design and dispute resolution.

Structural Distress Assessment

Evaluation of cracking, settlement, heave, and differential movement in existing structures — with root cause identification and remediation guidance.

Pavement Engineering

Subgrade evaluation, pavement design, distress investigation, and rehabilitation recommendations for roadways, parking structures, and airfields.

Forensic & Expert Services

When the Cause Matters as Much as the Fix

Geoneering has extensive experience providing forensic investigation services to insurance carriers, law firms, property owners, and contractors. Our expert witnesses are licensed Professional Engineers with courtroom and deposition experience, and our findings are always grounded in sound methodology and documented evidence.

We handle cases ranging from single-family foundation disputes to large-scale commercial construction defect litigation, always with the goal of delivering clear, technically defensible conclusions.

  • Root Cause Investigation

    Systematic analysis to determine the origin and contributing factors of structural distress, failures, or property damage.

  • Expert Reports & Testimony

    Peer-reviewed, well-documented reports prepared to withstand scrutiny — and expert witnesses who communicate technical findings clearly to judges and juries.

  • Mediation & Dispute Support

    Independent technical guidance for mediation proceedings, helping parties understand complex engineering issues before costly litigation.

  • Damage Assessment & Repair Scoping

    Detailed field assessment of damaged structures with repair cost estimates and remediation recommendations backed by engineering analysis.
