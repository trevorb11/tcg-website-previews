Our History

A Century of Building Southern California

1919
Charles W. Driver founds the company in Southern California with a simple creed: build it right, treat people fairly, and the rest takes care of itself.
1952
California's postwar population boom creates massive demand for schools. C.W. Driver builds dozens of campuses that are still serving students today.
1975
First healthcare project completed under OSHPD regulations. A specialized practice area is born that will define the firm for the next 50 years.
1994
After the Northridge earthquake, C.W. Driver mobilizes a seismic retrofit program that restores hundreds of structures and earns the region's lasting trust.
2008
First LEED Platinum project delivered. The firm's commitment to sustainability becomes a competitive differentiator, not just a checkbox.
Today
$8B+ delivered. 87% repeat client rate. 300+ people who show up every day because they are proud of what this company stands for.
