Our Founder

Dr. Marilyn P. Wiles

President & Chief Executive Officer

Marilyn Wiles didn't come to consulting to be comfortable. She came because she'd spent a career — from Vietnam-era Air Force service to command leadership to the Stanford Graduate School of Business — learning exactly what separates high-performing organizations from struggling ones. And she'd seen too many agencies and companies settle for less than they were capable of.

A retired Air Force Colonel with a doctorate in Systems Engineering from UCLA, Dr. Wiles was selected as a Sloan Fellow at Stanford — a distinction reserved for executives identified as future leaders of major organizations. She took those credentials and that conviction and founded Enlighteneering in 2001 in McLean, Virginia, five miles from the agencies she set out to serve.

Today, she leads a network of more than 300 coaches and consultants across the country — every one of them selected because they can do what Marilyn does: walk into an organization, understand what's really happening, and help leaders rise to meet it.
