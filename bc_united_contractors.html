About BC United Contractors

Built on the West Bank. Built on trust. BC United Contractors is a general contracting company based in Avondale, Louisiana, proudly serving homeowners across Jefferson Parish and the greater New Orleans metro area.

Founded by Jackoby Bertot, BC United Contractors was born from a simple idea: that West Bank families deserve a contractor who shows up on time, communicates clearly, and delivers work they can be proud of. Jackoby brings hands-on experience across every trade — from framing and roofing to finish carpentry and tile work.

As a locally owned business with deep roots in the community, we understand what Louisiana homes need. We know the weather, we know the building codes, and we know what materials hold up in this climate. That local knowledge makes all the difference.
