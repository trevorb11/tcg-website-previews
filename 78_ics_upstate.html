The Challenge

Technology That Works Against You — Instead of For You

Most growing businesses reach a point where IT becomes a source of stress rather than a competitive advantage. Sound familiar?

Ransomware, phishing, and data breaches are targeting organizations of every size. Without proactive security, a single incident can cost far more than years of IT investment.

Unreliable Systems

Downtime costs money, productivity, and customer trust. Aging infrastructure and reactive break-fix support leave you vulnerable to the next failure.

Internal IT Stretched Too Thin

Your internal IT person — if you have one — is juggling too much. Strategic projects, user support, security, and vendor management can't all get the attention they need from one person.

About ICS Upstate

Rooted in Upstate New York for Over 30 Years

ICS Upstate has been serving organizations across the Southern Tier and Central New York for more than three decades — making it one of the region's largest privately held technology managed services firms.

What started as a local technology services business has grown into a team of nearly 150 experts operating from four offices — Endicott, East Syracuse, Ithaca, and Auburn. That growth reflects the trust that businesses across the region have placed in ICS over the years.

Joshua Wamser leads ICS Upstate with a straightforward philosophy: technology is supposed to make your business run better. When it doesn't, that's our problem to fix — proactively, locally, and without the runaround that clients get from impersonal national vendors.
