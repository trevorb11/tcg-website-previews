The Idea That Made Sergii Forbes 30 Under 30

Oil and gas producers have a problem: billions of cubic feet of natural gas are flared at well sites every year because there's no economical way to capture it. It gets burned off into the atmosphere — wasted energy and needless emissions, site after site across North America.

Sergii Gerasymovych saw something others missed. Bitcoin miners need cheap electricity. Oil producers have gas they can't use. The EZ Smartgrid Flaring Mitigation System is what happens when you connect those two facts. A mobile unit drives onto the well pad, converts the flared gas into electricity, and powers on-site Bitcoin mining — no infrastructure investment required from the producer.

The producer gets a new revenue stream from gas they were previously torching. Emissions drop by up to 70%. Miners get power at rates that make the economics of mining work. It's genuinely brilliant, and it works.

Sergii Gerasymovych: Forbes 30 Under 30, and the Person Behind EZ Smartgrid

Sergii co-founded EZ Blockchain in Chicago in 2017. He was 20-something years old, and his idea — that flared natural gas from oil wells could power Bitcoin mining — struck most people as eccentric. It turned out to be exactly right.

The math made sense and the environmental case was undeniable: flaring is one of the most visible and avoidable sources of methane emissions in the energy industry. Sergii's EZ Smartgrid FMS captures that gas before it burns, turns it into electricity, and puts it to work. No wasted energy. No wasted capital. Just a system that benefits the producer, the miner, and the atmosphere simultaneously.

In 2021, Forbes recognized Sergii in their 30 Under 30 Europe list for Manufacturing and Industry. He was also named among the Top 100 USA Entrepreneurs with Ukrainian Origin. He runs EZ Blockchain out of downtown Chicago, where the company continues to expand its infrastructure and forge new partnerships in the oil patch and beyond.
