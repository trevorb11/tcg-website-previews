Who We Are

Expert Safety Consulting That Goes Beyond Compliance

Progressive Safety Management was built on a conviction that workplace safety is not a checkbox — it is a fundamental commitment to the people who show up and do the work every day. Led by Daniel Deller, a safety professional with over 23 years of experience and credentials that set the standard in the industry, we provide safety and risk management services that actually change cultures.

Our team serves construction, general industry, manufacturing, and staffing clients across the Mid-Atlantic, Southeast, and Midwest. We don't believe in one-size-fits-all safety programs. We understand your risks, your operations, and your workforce — and we build solutions that fit your reality.

We benchmark lessons learned across the industries we serve, bringing best practices to every client regardless of their size — from 11 employees to 10,000.

What We Offer

Comprehensive Safety and Risk Management Services

Every service we offer is grounded in real-world field experience, regulatory expertise, and the understanding that effective safety programs protect people first — and business results follow.

Dedicated Safety Manager Services

An experienced safety professional embedded in your operations — on-site or part-time — providing continuous oversight, training, and compliance management without the cost of a full-time hire.

Safety Audits and Inspections

Comprehensive jobsite and facility audits that identify hazards, assess program effectiveness, and provide prioritized corrective action recommendations.

OSHA Compliance Training

Regulatory-compliant safety training programs tailored to your workforce, including OSHA 10 and 30-hour construction and general industry courses.

Written Safety Programs

Custom-developed safety and health programs that meet OSHA requirements and reflect the real conditions of your workplace — not generic templates.

OSHA Consultation Services

Pre-inspection preparation, response to citations, and ongoing regulatory compliance guidance so you're always ready for OSHA scrutiny.

Incident Investigation

Thorough root cause analysis following workplace incidents, with actionable corrective actions designed to prevent recurrence and protect your organization.

Safety Culture Development

Leadership training, behavioral safety programs, and employee engagement initiatives that build a genuine safety-first culture from the ground up.

Return-to-Work Programs

Modified duty and return-to-work program development that reduces lost time costs while supporting injured employees through recovery.
