Who We Are

Built on 40 Years of Integrity

Founded in 1985, ECA Marketing has spent four decades doing one thing: helping independent agents grow their book of business. We have stayed independent because that is what is best for our agents and, ultimately, for their clients. No quotas. No pressure. Just support.

We operate in all 50 states and several U.S. territories. Whether you are building a local practice or serving clients across the country, ECA gives you the carrier access, products, and back-office support to do it well.

Joe Spillman has led ECA as President since December 2011. Joe came in with a clear focus on product selection for complex estate and retirement planning situations, and that philosophy shapes everything we do. When you call ECA, there is a real chance Joe picks up.

"Our job is to make it easier for good advisors to do great work for their clients. Everything we do flows from that." — Joe Spillman, President

Our Approach

Designed Around Client Outcomes

Joe Spillman joined ECA in 2011 with one clear belief: product selection should be driven by what materially improves the client's outcome, especially in complex estate and retirement situations. Not by what is easiest to sell.

That belief still drives every carrier relationship, every product we add to the shelf, and every training session we run. We help agents build real confidence in solutions that serve clients well, because that is what builds lasting practices.

The new ECA media production studio takes that further, giving agents the branded content and client-facing tools to stand out in a crowded market without spending a fortune on marketing agencies.
