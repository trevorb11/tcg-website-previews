About Us

Southern California's Go-To Warehouse Partner

Warehouse Solutions Inc was founded in 2008 in Lakeside, California — the heart of San Diego County. From the start, we built the company around a simple idea: warehouses work better when the people designing and equipping them actually understand operations, not just sales.

Mark Wallace, our president and a Certified Lean, Six Sigma Black Belt Systems Engineer, leads a team with over 50 years of combined experience in material handling system design and installation. We don't just sell racks — we assess your space, design a custom solution, handle permits and engineering, install everything, and make sure it works the way your operation needs it to.

Recognized by Inc. Magazine as the 5th fastest growing company in San Diego County and 152nd nationally, we've built that growth by becoming the partner warehouse managers call when they need it done right — and when they need it done without disrupting operations.

Equipment

Every System,
New or Used

We carry the full range of industrial storage solutions — from basic selective racking to high-density drive-in systems — in new and quality used condition.

Most Popular

Selective Pallet Racking

The workhorse of most warehouses. Direct access to every pallet position. Available in teardrop and structural configurations for any load requirement.

Long & Wide Items

Cantilever Racking

Ideal for lumber, pipe, furniture, and other long or oversized items that don't fit on standard shelving. Available in light and heavy duty configurations.

High Density

Drive-In Racking

Maximize storage density for homogeneous SKUs. Forklifts drive into the rack lanes, supporting LIFO inventory management at high cube efficiency.

Space Efficient

Push-Back Racking

LIFO storage with multiple pallet positions deep per lane. Gravity-fed carts make loading and retrieval efficient without requiring forklifts to enter the rack.

FIFO / Flow

Pallet Flow Systems

First-in, first-out storage using gravity-fed rollers. Ideal for food, beverage, and date-sensitive inventory where rotation discipline is critical.

Vertical Storage

Mezzanines & Platforms

Use the vertical space you're paying for. Structural mezzanine systems create a second level of storage or workspace within your existing building footprint.
