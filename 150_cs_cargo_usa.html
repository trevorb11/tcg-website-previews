Our Story

Laredo’s Gateway Position,
Your Competitive Advantage

CS Cargo USA LLC operates at the heart of the world’s busiest land port — Laredo, Texas. Strategically positioned at 8402 El Gato Road, we leverage decades of cross-border expertise developed within the CARMI Logistics Group since 1983.

What began as a Mexican customs brokerage operation has grown into a full-service international logistics provider. Today, CS Cargo USA serves manufacturers, importers, exporters, and distributors moving product through the US-Mexico corridor under T-MEC/USMCA trade rules.

Located in Laredo, TX — #1 US land port by trade volume
Part of the CARMI Logistics Group, licensed since 1983
US National Customs License & Mexican Customs Broker Patent
PANGEA network member for global maritime forwarding reach
Active IATA member for international air cargo shipments
