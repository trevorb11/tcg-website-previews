About Measutronics

One Team, Everything You Need — Backed by Duncan-Parnell

Measutronics is the marine positioning and survey systems division of Duncan-Parnell, one of the Southeast's most respected survey and infrastructure technology companies. That backing gives us the resources of a large organization with the focus of a specialized team that does one thing exceptionally well.

We have been solving marine positioning problems for over 30 years. Our engineers and field specialists understand what your crew actually faces: connectors that must survive saltwater and vibration, displays readable in direct sunlight, and support people who can troubleshoot at 5 a.m. on a barge in a tidal river without needing to call anyone back at the office.

  • Complete system design from sensor selection through operator display
  • GNSS, optical, and acoustic positioning technologies
  • Multibeam and single-beam hydrographic survey integration
  • Dredge production monitoring and reporting systems
  • Marine construction verification and compliance documentation
