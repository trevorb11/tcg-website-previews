Our Story

A Mission Born from Seeing the Gap

2021 Founded

"I started RNR 365 because I saw too many people miss critical medical appointments. Dialysis patients. Veterans. Elderly neighbors. People who needed to be somewhere and had no safe way to get there. That's not a transportation problem — that's a care problem."

Rola Omar founded RNR 365 Solutions LLC in 2021, bringing over a decade of experience in non-emergency medical transportation to Corpus Christi and the surrounding South Texas region. As a woman-owned business and passionate veteran advocate, Rola built this company on a simple belief: everyone deserves access to the healthcare they need, and getting there should never be the obstacle.

Today, RNR 365 operates around the clock — every day of the year — providing ambulatory, wheelchair, stretcher, VA, and Medicaid transportation to patients who count on us to be there, on time, every time.

What We Offer

Transportation Built Around Your Needs

Every patient is different. We offer the right vehicle, equipment, and level of assistance to fit your situation — from a brief clinic visit to a complex specialist appointment.

Ambulatory Transport

For patients who are able to walk with assistance. Our trained drivers provide door-to-door service, helping you in and out of the vehicle safely and with dignity.

For patients who walk independently or with a cane or walker

Wheelchair Transport

Our wheelchair-accessible vehicles are equipped with hydraulic lifts and secure tie-down systems, ensuring smooth, safe transport for passengers who use wheelchairs or scooters.

For patients using manual or power wheelchairs

Stretcher Transport

Designed for patients who must remain in a lying-down position throughout the ride. Our stretcher vehicles are staffed and equipped to handle this level of care with professionalism.

For patients unable to sit upright during transport

VA Transportation

As a VA-certified transportation provider, we are proud to serve veterans who need rides to VA medical centers, community clinics, and other approved healthcare appointments.

For veterans with VA benefits or VA-authorized transportation needs

Medicaid Transportation

RNR 365 is an approved Medicaid transportation provider. Qualifying patients can access our services through their Medicaid benefit at no out-of-pocket cost for covered trips.

For patients with active Texas Medicaid benefits
