Our Products

Custom High-Pressure Forming
Equipment

Procurement managers and process engineers come to Loomis because standard equipment doesn't meet their tolerances. We don't sell off-the-shelf presses. We start with your material, your pressure requirements, and your production volume — then we build the machine your process actually needs.

Isostatic Press

Dry Bag Isostatic Press

Our dry bag isostatic presses provide uniform, all-around pressure to compact powdered materials into near-net-shape components — ideal for high-volume production environments where speed and consistency are critical.

Applications: Ceramic insulators, spark plugs, cutting tools, hard metal parts
Isostatic Press

Wet Bag Isostatic Press

Wet bag presses deliver exceptional flexibility for custom shapes and complex geometries. The flexible tooling conforms to any shape, making this approach ideal for prototype work, specialty parts, and low-to-medium production volumes.

Applications: Advanced ceramics, metal powder compaction, specialty materials
Extrusion

Piston / Ram Extruder

Our piston and ram extruders handle plastic powders, ceramics, and other materials that cannot be processed with conventional screw extruders. Available in a wide range of bore sizes and force capacities, configured to your material and output requirements.

Capacity Range: 16 tons to 1,200+ tons; custom bore and die configurations

Materials & Applications

Built for the
Most Demanding Materials

Our History

A Century of
Precision Engineering

Loomis Products has been building high-pressure forming equipment since 1917 — before most of the industries we serve even existed. In 1970, Karl Kahlefeld acquired the company and moved it to Levittown, Pennsylvania, where it operates today. In 1990 he founded Kahlefeld GmbH in Germany to serve growing European demand. Over a century later, the machines are more sophisticated, but the approach hasn't changed: talk to the engineer, understand the process, build exactly what's needed.

1917

Founded as a High-Pressure Forming Pioneer

Loomis Products Company is established, becoming one of the earliest practitioners of isostatic pressing technology in the United States.

1970

Acquisition & Relocation to Levittown, PA

Karl Kahlefeld acquires Loomis Products and relocates operations to the current facility at 5500 Bristol Emilie Road in Levittown, Pennsylvania, where the company continues to operate today.

1990

European Expansion

Karl Kahlefeld founds Loomis Products Kahlefeld GmbH in Germany, establishing a dedicated European manufacturing and service operation to serve growing international demand.

Today

Serving Global Customers

With over a century of expertise and operations on two continents, Loomis Products continues to engineer and manufacture custom isostatic presses and extrusion equipment for customers in North America, Europe, and Asia.
