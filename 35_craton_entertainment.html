Our Story

Built by an Entrepreneur
Who Loves Brands

Charles T. Craton III started Specialty Atlanta in 1988 out of Bogart, Georgia with a simple conviction: a brand's merchandise should feel like the brand, not like an afterthought. He'd seen too many companies hand out forgettable junk with their logo on it and wonder why nobody cared.

Over the next three-plus decades, Charles built that conviction into one of the most respected promotional products and motorsports merchandise operations in the Southeast. The company grew into Craton Promotions, then expanded its motorsports division into Craton Entertainment — now the official merchandise partner for IMSA and multiple premier racing venues.

"Every item someone picks up with your logo on it is a decision about your brand. We take that seriously — and we build programs where that decision always works in your favor."

— Charles T. Craton III, Founder

Today, Craton is headquartered in the Athens, Georgia area and serves clients nationally — from family-owned businesses building their first company store to motorsports venues managing six-figure race-day merchandise operations. Charles is involved in every relationship.
