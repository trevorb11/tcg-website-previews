About GetMeAV

50 Years of Experience. One Number to Call.

Astoria Communications was founded in 1996 in Astoria, New York, with a simple vision: make world-class audiovisual solutions accessible and seamless for any client, any venue, any event. With over 50 years of combined experience across our team, we've been involved in thousands of events, installations, and productions throughout the country.

What started as a New York-based consulting AV firm grew into a full-service operation with a thriving Florida division. We expanded to serve our New York clients hosting winter conferences in Florida, and stayed because the market was there and we were good at what we do.

Today, GetMeAV is the single point of contact for audio, video, lighting, staging, and permanent AV installations for clients across the hospitality, corporate, education, entertainment, and house of worship sectors.
