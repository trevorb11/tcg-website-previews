What We Do

From the Assembly Line
to the Dealer Lot

Every vehicle that rolls off a plant floor needs to reach a dealer in perfect condition, on time, with a documented chain of custody. That is the discipline Precision Vehicle Solutions is built around — and it is all we do.

01
Finished Vehicle Logistics
Moving new vehicles from automotive assembly plants to dealerships across the distribution network. Fleet managers and plant operations directors rely on documented chain of custody, damage-free delivery protocols, and FMCSA-compliant carrier operations — every vehicle, every route.
  • Assembly plant to dealership transport
  • OEM direct distribution support
  • Multi-make and multi-model capability
  • Damage-free delivery protocols
  • Chain-of-custody documentation
  • FMCSA-compliant carrier operations
02
Yard Management & Processing
On-site yard management at manufacturing facilities and distribution centers. When throughput velocity and OEM compliance standards are non-negotiable, plant operations directors need a partner who runs the yard like it’s their own.
  • Vehicle yard operations management
  • Pre-delivery inspection (PDI) support
  • Vehicle staging and sequencing
  • Inventory tracking and reporting
  • Damage assessment and documentation
  • Surface solutions (via FWL Surface Solutions)
03
Rail Transportation Support
Industrial railcar switching and rail transportation support coordinated through our FLEXRail platform. For operations where rail is part of the distribution equation, PVS provides the integration point between plant rail sidings and the road network.
  • Industrial railcar switching services
  • Rail-to-road transition coordination
  • Automotive rail siding operations
  • FLEXRail platform (sister brand)
  • Plant rail operations support
  • Intermodal logistics coordination
04
OEM & Dealer Network Support
Integrated logistics across the OEM-to-dealer supply chain, backed by the full Precision Vehicle Holdings network. AutoVentive provides the technology layer; PVS and its sister brands provide the operational execution across multi-location footprints in Michigan, Ohio, Georgia, and beyond.
  • OEM manufacturer partnerships
  • Dealer network delivery coordination
  • AutoVentive platform integration
  • Multi-location operations (MI, OH, GA)
  • Volume throughput scalability
  • Compliance reporting and documentation

Why Precision Matters

Built Into the
Automotive Supply Chain

Finished vehicle logistics is a specialized discipline. OEMs and their tier-one partners cannot afford a logistics provider that is learning the business. Precision Vehicle Holdings operates where vehicles are built — near the plants, inside the yards, on the sidings — with the compliance record and network depth to match.

01
DOT-Registered Carrier — USDOT #3288980
Active registration in the FMCSA SAFER system, publicly verifiable. Fleet managers and compliance teams can confirm carrier credentials before a single vehicle moves. That accountability is not optional in this industry — it is the baseline.
02
Positioned at the Plant, Not the Periphery
Operations near major automotive manufacturing plants in Michigan, Ohio, and Georgia. When a plant director needs a logistics partner, proximity and familiarity with the facility’s rhythm matters as much as capacity.
03
Six Brands. One Integrated Network.
As part of Precision Vehicle Holdings, PVS draws on sister brands across trucking (Precision Trucking Solutions), rail (FLEXRail), surface treatment (FWL Surface Solutions), and logistics technology (AutoVentive). A single point of contact for multi-modal automotive logistics needs.
04
OEM-Grade Documentation and Standards
Major manufacturers require documentation, performance standards, and compliance that most logistics providers cannot deliver. PVS structures its operations around the requirements of the OEM environment — because that is the environment we operate in every day.
