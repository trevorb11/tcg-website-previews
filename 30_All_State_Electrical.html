Full-Service Commercial Electrical

New construction, renovation, industrial fit-out, design-build — All-State does it all. Jacob Patton is a Georgia Tech graduate, and that engineering mindset shows in how his team approaches every project: methodically, safely, and without cutting corners. Same quality on a tenant build-out as on a full industrial build.

🏗

New Construction Electrical

Complete electrical systems for commercial and industrial new construction from rough-in through final connection. Coordinated with all other trades throughout the build.

🔧

Commercial Renovation

Tenant build-outs, office renovations, lighting upgrades, and panel replacements. We work in occupied facilities with minimum disruption to your operations.

⚙

Industrial Electrical

Motor controls, equipment power feeds, 3-phase distribution, and industrial automation wiring. We understand the demands of production environments.

Emergency Service
