How We Work

Clear Process. No Surprises.

Construction should be straightforward. Our process keeps every project on track, on budget, and fully communicated from the first call to the final walkthrough.

01

Consultation

We visit your site, listen to your goals, and assess the scope of work. No assumptions, no generic estimates — we want to understand your project first.

02

Proposal

A detailed written proposal with scope of work, materials, timeline, and pricing. Everything is itemized and explained so there are no hidden costs or surprises.

03

Build

Our expert crews get to work. Jonathan Chang maintains hands-on oversight throughout construction, and we keep you updated at every major milestone.

04

Completion

We conduct a full walkthrough with you before we consider the job done. Punch list items are resolved promptly, and we don't leave until you're satisfied.

About CTI Construction

Built on Experience. Driven by Quality.

CTI Construction Inc has been delivering residential and commercial projects across the New York metro area since 2010. What started as a focused residential contracting operation has grown into a full-service general contractor trusted by homeowners, commercial property owners, and developers across five boroughs and beyond.

Jonathan Chang, President of CTI Construction, brings more than 20 years of hands-on industry experience to every project. His approach is personal — he is not a contractor who wins the bid and disappears. Jonathan is involved in project oversight from the first consultation through final inspection, ensuring that the quality and professionalism he promises at the start is what you see at the finish.
