What We Do

Electrical Services

From a single circuit in a Scarsdale colonial to a full commercial buildout in White Plains, our licensed team knows this region's buildings, its inspectors, and its code requirements. We've been doing this work here for years.

R

Residential

Whether it's a knob-and-tube house in Tarrytown or a new build in Ossining, we know Westchester homes. Licensed, insured, and thorough — every time.

  • Panel upgrades & replacements
  • Outlet & switch installation
  • Ceiling fans & fixtures
  • EV charger installation
  • Whole-home rewiring
  • Generator installation
C

Commercial

We know Westchester and NYC commercial code cold — and we work alongside general contractors who need a sub they can count on to show up, perform, and pass inspection the first time.

  • Commercial wiring & buildouts
  • Lighting design & installation
  • Three-phase power systems
  • Code compliance inspections
  • Security & access systems
  • Data & low-voltage cabling
E

Emergency

We pick up. Day or night. Electrical hazards don't wait for business hours, and neither do we. When something feels wrong, call — we'll tell you if it's urgent and get someone there.

  • Power outage diagnosis & repair
  • Tripped breaker & panel issues
  • Burning smell / sparking
  • Storm damage restoration
  • Immediate safety shutoffs
  • Same-day service available
I

Inspections

Buying a 1960s Cape in Mount Kisco? Don't trust the general inspector for electrical. We find what others miss — and document it in a way your attorney and insurance company will understand.

  • Home buyer inspections
  • Smoke & CO detector review
  • AFCI/GFCI compliance
  • Older home assessments
  • Insurance documentation
  • Code violation remediation
