Our Story

Built for the Markets Big Machines Overlooked

MacTec Packaging Technologies was founded in 2004 in Sayreville, New Jersey after identifying a real gap in the packaging equipment market: the companies that needed small-to-medium output unit dose machines were being forced to buy large-scale equipment they didn't need, or accept compromised alternatives.

We built machines designed specifically for this production range — fast, flexible, easy to operate, and engineered in the USA. Over the last two decades, that focus has made us the trusted partner for pharmaceutical manufacturers, nutraceutical producers, R&D labs, and a growing range of specialty industries.

2004

MacTec Packaging Technologies founded in Sayreville, NJ — filling a gap in small-to-medium output packaging machinery.

2013

Introduction of the K-Cup Monobloc Filler — expanding into the fast-growing single-serve coffee and beverage market.

2020

Relocated to a larger New Jersey facility to meet increasing demand and expand production capacity.

2023

Strengthened leadership team to support continued growth across pharmaceutical, nutraceutical, and specialty markets.

Product Lines

Three Core Machine Types. Infinite Configurations.

Every MacTec machine is designed to be operator-friendly, compact, and fast to changeover — so your production team spends time producing, not troubleshooting.

Blister Packaging Machines

From table-top units to full-scale blister packaging lines, MacTec's blister machines handle unit dose pharmaceutical, nutraceutical, and consumer health products with speed and precision.

  • Speeds exceeding 100 products per minute
  • Quick tooling changeover — minutes by one operator
  • Compatible with cold-form and thermoform materials
  • Table-top to full-production scale options
  • GMP-compatible design

Pouch Packaging Solutions

Flexible sachet and pouch packaging equipment for powders, granules, liquids, and solid dose products. Adjustable for multiple formats and product types within the same machine.

  • Sachet, stick pack, and pouch formats
  • Compatible with heat-seal and specialty laminated films
  • Adjustable fill weight and format width
  • Nutraceutical, pharmaceutical, and food-grade applications
  • Compact footprint for limited production space

Rotary Filling Equipment

High-speed rotary filling monoblocks for capsules, tablets, and single-serve formats including the K-Cup compatible filler. Designed for reliable, high-throughput continuous production.

  • Monobloc filling systems with integrated sealing
  • K-Cup and single-serve coffee/beverage compatible
  • Continuous rotary motion for consistent output
  • Bottle, capsule, and pod filling configurations
  • Made in USA, service-backed
