Our Programs

Healthcare Certifications That
Employers Are Hiring For

Short-term, hands-on programs designed to get you working in healthcare fast. Most programs complete in weeks, not years.

Certified Nursing Assistant (CNA)

Learn essential patient care skills including bathing, feeding, vital signs, and communication with the healthcare team. Maryland State Board approved curriculum.

4–6 Weeks Enroll Now

Geriatric Nursing Assistant (GNA)

Advanced training focused on elder care in nursing homes and assisted living facilities. GNA certification is required for work in Maryland long-term care facilities.

6–8 Weeks Enroll Now

Home Health Aide (HHA)

Prepare to care for patients in their own homes. HHA training covers personal care, homemaking, safety, and working with elderly and disabled adults.

3–4 Weeks Enroll Now

Medication Technician (Med Tech)

Learn safe medication administration procedures for long-term care settings. This advanced credential increases your earning potential significantly.

3–5 Weeks Enroll Now

Patient Care Technician (PCT)

A comprehensive certification combining CNA skills with phlebotomy and EKG training. PCTs are in high demand at hospitals and outpatient clinics.

8–12 Weeks Enroll Now

CPR & First Aid Certification

American Heart Association-aligned CPR, AED, and first aid training for healthcare workers and individuals. Required for most healthcare employment.
