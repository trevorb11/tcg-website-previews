Who We Are

Built Around Listening. Delivered Through Design.

MKS Corp is a full-service architecture and engineering firm that approaches every project the same way: by listening first. We believe great design begins with a deep understanding of what a client actually needs — not what looks impressive in a portfolio.

Our team brings architectural design and structural engineering expertise under one roof, allowing us to develop solutions that are both visually compelling and technically sound. We work across commercial, institutional, and mixed-use sectors, with projects ranging from ground-up construction to adaptive reuse and renovation.

What sets us apart is our collaborative process. We involve clients at every stage — from programming and concept development through construction administration — so the finished project reflects their vision, not just ours.

Our Approach

A Process That Puts Clients First

We don't design in a vacuum. Our four-phase process is built around ongoing client engagement — from first conversation to the moment you open the doors.

01

Discovery and Programming

We listen deeply before we draw a line. This phase defines project goals, functional requirements, site constraints, and the vision that will guide the design.

02

Schematic Design

Concepts take form. We explore multiple design directions and engage clients in selecting the approach that best serves their needs and aspirations.

03

Design Development

The selected concept is refined. Structural systems, materials, and building systems are integrated. The design begins to look exactly like what you'll actually build.

04

Documents and Administration

Complete construction documents are issued. During construction, we provide administration support — answering questions, reviewing submittals, and ensuring the project is built as designed.
