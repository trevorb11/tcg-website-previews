Capabilities

Precision Machining Services

Full-spectrum machining capabilities for the most demanding aerospace and defense applications. Every part held to exacting tolerances.

CNC Machining (3 & 4 Axis)

Multi-axis CNC milling for complex aerospace geometries. Tight tolerances on aluminum, steel, titanium, and exotic alloys.

Jig Bore

Ultra-precise hole location and bore work for critical aerospace assemblies requiring micron-level positional accuracy.

Jig Grinding

Precision grinding of hardened components and tooling. Sub-micron surface finishes for critical mating surfaces.

Conventional Milling

Manual and semi-automatic milling for tooling, fixtures, and one-off parts where hands-on craftsmanship matters.

Conventional Turning

Lathe work for shafts, bushings, and cylindrical components. Reliable, repeatable results on manual turning centers.

Prototype Machining

Rapid single-piece and small-batch prototypes. Fast turnaround to support your design validation and testing cycles.

Production Runs

Scalable production machining with consistent quality. From dozens to thousands of parts with full traceability.

Complex Machining

Multi-operation parts with challenging geometries, tight tolerances, and exotic materials. We thrive on difficulty.

About Us

The Shop Behind the Parts

Founded on Precision. Driven by Commitment.

Wynn Precision, operating as TK Tooling & Manufacturing LLC, was founded in 2018 in Garden Grove, California. From day one, our mission has been straightforward: deliver aerospace-grade machined components on time, on spec, every time.

Our team of approximately eight dedicated machinists brings decades of combined experience across CNC machining, jig bore, jig grinding, and conventional machining methods. We operate 3-axis and 4-axis CNC equipment alongside manual mills and lathes, giving us the flexibility to handle everything from one-off prototypes to sustained production runs.

As a Woman-Owned Small Business (WOSB) and minority-owned enterprise, we bring supplier diversity value to prime contractors and government agencies. We have been registered on SAM.gov since 2020, hold CAGE Code 8HAL0, and have documented government contract experience.

We work with aerospace primes, defense contractors, aircraft manufacturers, and guided missile/space vehicle programs. When the application demands zero defects and absolute traceability, that is where Wynn Precision operates.
