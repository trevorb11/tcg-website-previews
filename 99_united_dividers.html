What We Manufacture

Built to Protect

United Dividers manufactures three core product types, each designed to keep your products separated, protected, and arrived in perfect condition.

Corrugated Partitions

Single-face and double-face corrugated partitions for high-strength protection in demanding shipping environments. Ideal for heavy items, automotive components, and industrial goods that need real structural separation between units.

Chipboard Dividers

Cost-effective chipboard dividers for applications where lighter-duty separation is sufficient. Widely used in food, beverage, and consumer goods packaging to keep products organized and prevent contact damage during handling.

Paperboard Pads

Flat paperboard pads and layer dividers for stacking applications, bottle packaging, and end-of-row protection. Available in a wide range of thicknesses and sizes, die-cut to your exact specifications.

Our Capabilities

Precision Manufacturing. Reliable Delivery.

United Dividers has built its reputation on two things: the quality of our products and the reliability of our delivery. Our seasoned team — many of whom have been with us for decades — calibrates our tooling to precise specifications to produce partitions that fit your containers and protect your products exactly as expected.

We maintain a fleet of trucks for floor-to-door delivery so that when your production line needs partitions, they arrive on time. Our just-in-time and kanban programs allow us to integrate directly into your supply chain and eliminate the inventory headaches that come with managing packaging materials in-house.

  • Precision tooling calibrated to your exact container specifications
  • Custom die-cutting for non-standard sizes and configurations
  • Fleet of trucks for floor-to-door delivery service
  • Just-in-time (JIT) and kanban supply programs available
  • Large-volume end users, container manufacturers, and brokers all welcome
  • Decades of experience across demanding industrial supply chains
