Welcome to Fripp Island

Where the Outside World Stays Outside

Private Gated Island
Barrier island community

Fripp Island is not a hotel. It's a private, gated barrier island community on South Carolina's Lowcountry coast -- a place where the word "resort" feels like an understatement. Access is by invitation, the pace is unhurried, and the Atlantic Ocean is the neighbor everyone came to see.

Nearly 3.5 miles of unspoiled beach greet you at sunrise and glow orange at dusk. Golf carts replace cars, wildlife shares the paths with joggers, and the only real decision most mornings is whether to start at the beach or the breakfast buffet.

This is where families return year after year. Where couples come to reconnect. Where companies come to rediscover what it feels like to think clearly. Fripp Island stays with you long after you've headed back to the mainland.

36 Holes of Coastal Golf at Its Finest

Fripp Island's two championship courses are designed to reward every level of player while delivering the kind of scenery that makes a double bogey feel like a victory lap. Ocean breezes, marsh views, and coastal wildlife are your playing companions.

Golf programs for all skill levels are available, from clinics for beginners to personalized instruction for those looking to lower their handicap. Reserve tee times early -- the courses fill quickly during peak season.

On-Island Activities

There Is Always Something Worth Doing

Fripp Island is designed for people who want to be active, and equally designed for people who want to do absolutely nothing. Both are welcome here.

Beach and Water Sports

Kayak tours, dolphin cruises, paddleboard rentals, fishing charters, and surf equipment rentals make the Atlantic the center of activity for water lovers of every skill level.

Camelot Farms Equestrian

Guided horseback rides through Fripp Island's natural landscape offer a completely unique perspective on the island's beaches, marshes, and maritime forest.

Racquet Club

Tennis and pickleball courts for all skill levels, with clinics, open play, and organized events available throughout the season.

Nature Center

Interactive exhibits, guided wildlife tours, animal encounters, and specialty programs including Camp Fripp make the Nature Center a highlight for families and nature enthusiasts alike.

Marina and Boating

Fripp Island's full-service marina offers boat rentals, docking, fishing gear, and guided excursions out into the Lowcountry waterways.

Leisure and Wellness

Retail shops, salon services, cycling trails, and the island's own unhurried rhythm make Fripp Island equally suited to those who came specifically to slow down.
