Who We Are

Started in 2006. Built for Every Business.

Sheet Labels Inc. was founded in 2006 by Adam Gray with one purpose: make professional-quality labels accessible to businesses of every size. Adam was frustrated by a market where getting great labels meant either buying more than you needed, paying too much, or settling for quality that did not match your brand. He believed there was a better way — and built the company to prove it.

Nearly two decades later, the family legacy continues. Adam's son Larry Gray leads the team today, bringing the same commitment to craft and customer service that built the company from the ground up. Our 35,000 square foot facility in Glens Falls, New York and our Texas location now serve more than 100,000 active customers nationwide — from local food producers to Fortune 500 brands. Every label ships through a 12-step quality inspection process, manufactured right here in the United States.

Whether you need 25 labels or 25 million, you get the same attention to quality and the same real customer support team, available Monday through Friday.
