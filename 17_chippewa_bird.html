Year-Round Feeding

Seasonal Bird Feeding Guide

What to feed the birds of northeast Michigan through every season. Stop in and we’ll set you up with the right blend all year long.

Spring

Transition to high-protein blends as migrating species return. Add nyjer for goldfinches arriving in April. Put out hummingbird feeders by mid-May. Nesting birds need calcium — offer crushed eggshells.

Summer

Keep nectar feeders clean and fresh — change every 3 days in heat. Offer fruit for orioles and tanagers. Sunflower and safflower remain staples. Provide a water source for bathing.

Fall

Migration season — boost calories with peanuts and suet. Increase sunflower seed quantities as birds fatten up for winter. Watch for evening grosbeaks and pine siskins passing through.

Winter

High-fat feeds are critical. Load up on suet, peanuts, and black oil sunflower. Keep feeders full — birds depend on reliable food sources in northeast Michigan’s harsh winters. Add cracked corn for ground feeders.
