Our Story

Building Community in South Florida

Sun Group Development is a Miami Lakes-based real estate development firm with deep roots in the South Florida community. We develop residential communities and mixed-use properties that are designed not just to be places to live — but places to belong.

From the earliest land acquisition through design, permitting, construction, and delivery, our team maintains hands-on involvement at every stage. We believe that the quality of what we build reflects the values of who we are — and we take that seriously.

Located at the heart of Miami Lakes, we have the local knowledge, regulatory expertise, and community relationships to navigate South Florida's dynamic real estate environment with confidence and vision.

Our Philosophy

The Principles Behind Every Project

Every development we undertake is guided by a set of commitments that run deeper than design specs and building codes.

01

Quality Construction

We select materials and contractors that reflect our commitment to durability, safety, and craftsmanship — building homes and spaces that stand the test of time.

02

Community First

Great developments strengthen the communities they enter. We engage with neighborhoods, preserve character, and create spaces where people genuinely want to live and gather.

03

Sustainable Design

South Florida's environment is extraordinary. We design with sustainability in mind — energy efficiency, native landscaping, and responsible land use across all our projects.

04

Local Expertise

The South Florida market is unique. Our intimate knowledge of Miami-Dade and Broward County — from zoning to market trends — gives our projects a meaningful competitive edge.
