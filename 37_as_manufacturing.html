Metal Stamping

CNC Milling

Our Story

Started With One Press.
Kept the Same Standard.

A&S Manufacturing was founded in 1991 in a 5,000 square foot building in Philadelphia with a single punch press and a clear philosophy: build it right, stand behind it, and the business will follow.

Steve Churchill's inspiration came from watching a family member in the fastener sales business. He remembers thinking: these screws have to go somewhere. Someone has to stamp the bracket, machine the housing, build the frame. So he became that someone — right here in Philadelphia — and he has been building it ever since.

"We started with one press and a promise. We've kept both."

Steve Churchill, Owner — A&S Manufacturing Co

Today, A&S employs a team of seasoned machinists and tool and die makers with over 60 years of combined experience. The company is ISO 9001:2015 certified, ships to customers across the U.S. and into Mexico, and is currently positioned as a key supplier in the growing battery and energy storage industry.

And Steve Churchill still shows up every day. That has not changed in 35 years.
