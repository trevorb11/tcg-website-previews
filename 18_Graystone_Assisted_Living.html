Common Questions

Questions Families Ask Us

This is one of the most important decisions your family will make. We want you to have every answer you need — before, during, and after your visit.

What makes Graystone different from other assisted living?
Graystone is a 7,000 sq ft residential home with a maximum of 16 residents. It is not a facility or a campus. Residents live in a real house, on a quiet street, with caregivers who know them personally. The experience is fundamentally different from institutional care.
What does the monthly cost include?
Pricing starts at $4,100/month — well below Houston's average of $5,200. This covers accommodations, meals, personal care, daily activities, housekeeping, laundry, and 24-hour staffing availability. Contact us to discuss specific care needs and exact pricing.
Is Graystone licensed for memory care?
Yes. Graystone Life Care holds TX DADS Assisted Living License #103060 and Memory Care License #106303. We are Alzheimer's certified and licensed for non-ambulatory residents.
How many residents live at Graystone?
Graystone accommodates up to 16 residents. This intentionally small number ensures every resident receives personal attention and genuine care — not the assembly-line experience of a 100-bed facility.
Can families visit anytime?
Yes. We welcome family visits. Our home setting means visitors feel comfortable and at ease, not like they are entering an institution. We encourage families to stay involved in their loved one's daily life.
Where is Graystone located?
The facility is at 1310 Riverview Circle, Houston, TX 77077. Our administrative office is at 1502 Augusta, Suite 360, Houston, TX 77057. You can reach us at (713) 417-3840 or (346) 312-5600.
