End-to-End Telecom Network Delivery

TPRC Broadband is a service vendor to telecommunications companies and business users — handling every stage from design through commissioning with the precision and safety that network infrastructure demands.

01 / installation

Telecom Equipment Installation

Professional installation of telecommunications equipment at carrier facilities, central offices, and business sites — on spec and on schedule.

02 / fiber

Fiber Optic Cable Splicing

High-precision fiber optic splicing using current fusion and mechanical splicing techniques — ensuring low loss and long-term reliability in every connection.

03 / testing

Network Testing & Commissioning

Comprehensive network testing protocols — OTDR testing, optical power measurement, and full end-to-end verification before any circuit goes live.

04 / turnup

Service Turn-Up

Coordinated service turn-up with carrier provisioning teams — ensuring new circuits are tested, validated, and delivered on the date your customer expects.

05 / design

Network Design

Network design services for broadband expansion projects — including route planning, splice placement, and equipment specification for fiber deployments.

06 / pm

Project Management

End-to-end project management for complex telecom deployments — coordinating schedules, subcontractors, permitting, and carrier coordination from start to finish.

Building the Networks That Connect the World

TPRC Broadband is a Greensboro, North Carolina-based telecommunications contractor serving carriers, internet service providers, and enterprise clients across the country. Led by CEO Jason McElvey, our team of 44 skilled technicians and project professionals brings a depth of field experience that most providers simply cannot match.

We are a service vendor — not a carrier. That means our job is to make your network build happen correctly, efficiently, and safely. From rural broadband expansion projects to metro fiber builds and last-mile deployments, TPRC Broadband delivers the technical execution behind connectivity initiatives that matter.

Our team is OSHA-trained, safety-first, and committed to the kind of quality workmanship that ensures your network performs reliably for decades — not just on the day we finish the job.

Who We Work With

TPRC Broadband partners with organizations that build and operate the networks that keep people and businesses connected.

Telecommunications Carriers

Major and regional carriers requiring skilled subcontract crews for fiber deployment, equipment installation, and central office work across their service territories.

Internet Service Providers

Enterprise Businesses

Government & Municipal
