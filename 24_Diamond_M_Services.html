Our Story

Miranda Built This Business the Hard Way

Miranda, the founder of Diamond M Services, spent 13 years working FEMA disaster assistance — coordinating logistics, managing people, and making sure things got done right under pressure. When she started Diamond M in 2011, she brought all of that into a cleaning and property maintenance company built on process, reliability, and real accountability.

More than a decade later, Diamond M is one of Baldwin County's most trusted names for vacation rental cleaning, move-out services, and property maintenance. Miranda also operates Diamond M Properties LLC, which means she understands the short-term rental business from both sides. When she says she knows what property managers need, she means it.
