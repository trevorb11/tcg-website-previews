About HST Services

Built on Expertise. Trusted by the Medical Community.

H&S Technical Services was founded in 1998 with a single focus: deliver exceptional technical support for the dialysis and medical communities. For over two decades, we built our reputation on the quality of our work, the depth of our technicians' expertise, and our reliability when dialysis facilities and hospitals needed us most.

In 2020, H&S Technical Services joined the Nipro Medical Corporation family, becoming NIPRO Technical Solutions. The Nipro affiliation has expanded our capabilities and resources while preserving the same technical depth and customer commitment that defined HST from the beginning.

Today, we operate from our Mesa, Arizona facility, providing service and support for all major brands of hemodialysis machines and cardiovascular perfusion equipment throughout our service territory. Our NIST traceable calibration center rounds out a truly comprehensive technical services offering.

What We Do

Technical Services for Critical Medical Equipment

From hemodialysis machine repair to NIST-traceable calibration, HST provides the full technical service suite that dialysis facilities and hospitals need to keep their equipment running reliably.

Hemodialysis Machine Repair

Expert repair of circuit card assemblies and system modules for all major brands of hemodialysis machines. Our technicians have over thirty combined years of experience in dialysis equipment repair, and their diagnostic accuracy and repair quality reflect it. We minimize machine downtime to protect your patients and your operations.

Cardiovascular Perfusion Equipment

Technical support and repair services for a variety of cardiovascular perfusion equipment. We understand the critical role this equipment plays in patient care and approach every service engagement with the precision and urgency that these applications require.

Parts Services

Extensive parts inventory and sourcing expertise for all major hemodialysis brands. When you need a specific component to get a machine back online, our parts team finds it fast — reducing repair lead times and keeping your machines in service rather than waiting on supply chains.

NIST Traceable Calibration

Our full-service calibration center provides NIST traceable reference solutions for dialysis equipment — ensuring your machines meet accuracy standards required by clinical protocols and regulatory requirements. Calibration documentation is provided with every completed service.
