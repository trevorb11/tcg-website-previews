Our Philosophy

Dentistry That Feels Different

Established in 2010, Sea Island Dentistry has been the trusted dental home for families across Lady's Island, Beaufort, and the surrounding Lowcountry for over a decade. Dr. Kyle Farnsworth and our team believe that dentistry should be comfortable, modern, and completely judgment-free.

We know that many patients come to us with anxiety — maybe about past experiences, or things that weren't explained clearly, or just the general unease of sitting in a dental chair. We take that seriously. Our team is trained to put you at ease, explain every step before we take it, and move at a pace that's comfortable for you.

Your oral health is deeply connected to your overall wellbeing — heart disease, diabetes, and other systemic conditions all have links to oral health. We look at the full picture and communicate with your other healthcare providers when it's helpful to do so.

"We set out to put an end to every fear and unpleasant stereotype you've ever known about dentistry."

Advanced Technology

Modern Care from Day One

We invest in technology that makes your experience more comfortable, more accurate, and more efficient. Less guessing, less discomfort, better results.

Digital X-Rays

Sharper images with significantly less radiation. Instant results allow us to explain findings to you in real time.

3D Digital Impressions

No messy molds. Digital impressions are more accurate, more comfortable, and speed up your restorations.

Same-Day CEREC Crowns

Your crown is designed and milled in-office while you wait. One appointment, one visit, done.

Dental Lasers

Less invasive soft tissue treatment. Less discomfort, faster healing, and better precision than traditional methods.

Meet Your Dentist

Dr. Kyle J. Farnsworth

Doctor of Dental Medicine — Lady's Island, SC

Dr. Kyle Farnsworth has been dedicated to dentistry for over 20 years. He founded Sea Island Dentistry in 2010 with a clear vision: to build a practice where patients feel genuinely cared for, where the technology is state-of-the-art, and where the quality of every restoration is something patients can be proud of — for years to come.

Trained at the Medical University of South Carolina and with additional graduate post-residency training at the University of Utah, Dr. Farnsworth brings an exceptional depth of clinical education to his practice in the Beaufort area. He stays current with continuing education in implants, cosmetic dentistry, sleep apnea therapy, and advanced restorative techniques.

Patients describe him as warm, thorough, and patient — someone who takes the time to explain what he's doing and why, and who treats every patient with the same care he'd give a family member.
