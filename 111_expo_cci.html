Who We Are

A Family Business Built on Trust and Craftsmanship

Since 1990, Expo Convention Contractors has been the name exhibitors across the country turn to when the stakes are high and the details matter. We started as a family operation in Miami, and that personal touch is still at the heart of everything we do — every exhibit, every event, every client relationship.

We've grown into one of Florida's most respected full-service general contractors for tradeshows, conventions, conferences, and special events, with offices in Miami, Los Angeles, and Medellin, Colombia. But no matter the scale of your project, you still deal with people who care.

From concept through installation and beyond, we handle every detail in-house — floor plans, fabrication, graphics, material handling, and logistics — so nothing falls through the cracks on your show day.

  • Family-owned and operated for over three decades
  • Full in-house capabilities from design through delivery
  • Trusted by Fortune 500 companies, associations, and first-time exhibitors
  • Licensed general contractor with a proven track record

How We Work

From First Conversation to Final Installation

We've refined our process over 30 years to make your experience as smooth as the exhibits we build.

Consultation and Discovery

We learn your goals, timeline, budget, and brand story. There's no cookie-cutter approach here — every engagement starts with genuine listening.

Design and Concept

Our in-house designers develop concepts tailored to your space, objectives, and aesthetic — with revisions until it's exactly right.

Fabrication and Print

Everything is built and printed in our own facilities — no outsourcing, no surprises. Quality is controlled at every step.

Installation and Show Support

Our crews handle installation on-site. We're there with you through show day so you can focus on your clients, not the logistics.

Post-Show Logistics

We manage dismantle, storage, and freight return so the end of the show is as effortless as the opening day.
