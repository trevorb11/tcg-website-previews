Our Story

Connecting People & Organizations Through Language

International Language Connection (ILC) is a team of dedicated language professionals based in Indianapolis, IN, committed to bridging communication gaps and building cultural understanding. Founded by John Cannaday, ILC was built on the belief that language is the most powerful tool for human connection.

Located at 719 Virginia Ave #101, we serve students, families, healthcare providers, legal professionals, and businesses throughout the Indianapolis metro area. Our instructors are native or near-native speakers with formal training in language education and translation.

Whether you need to learn a new language for personal growth, require certified translation for official documents, or want to prepare your team for international business, ILC delivers results with warmth, professionalism, and cultural sensitivity.
