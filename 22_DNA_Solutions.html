About DNA Solutions

Founded by Engineers.
Run Like a Partnership.

Constantine (Dean) Hadjis founded DNA Solutions in 2015 after years watching organizations struggle with IT vendors who over-promised and under-delivered. His background spans IT leadership and financial operations — which means he understands that every technology decision is also a business decision.

We call ourselves "Technological Hospitality Specialists" because we believe you should feel like a valued partner at every step — not a support ticket. Our office is in Bel Air, MD, and we serve organizations across the Baltimore region and the broader DMV corridor.
