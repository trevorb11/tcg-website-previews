About Jennifer

When You Call,
A Real Person Answers

Jennifer Altman founded Palmetto PC Solutions in Cayce, South Carolina in 2009. Her approach has always been the same: treat every customer the way she would want to be treated when she is the one who is frustrated and confused. With respect, plain language, and never any pressure to spend more than they need to.

She built Palmetto PC around a simple observation: most people call a computer repair shop because they are already stressed. The last thing they need is jargon and attitude. They need someone who listens, explains what is wrong, fixes it, and charges a fair price.

"Technology should make your life easier. When it stops doing that, just tell us what's going on — we'll take it from there."

Walk-ins are always welcome at our Cayce storefront on Memorial Drive. If you would rather call first, Jennifer or a member of her team picks up. It really is that simple.
