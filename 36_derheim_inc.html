About Troy

From Army Veteran to
Award-Winning Pool Builder

Troy Derheim came to Fargo in 1991 after an honorable discharge from the U.S. Army, having served in Operation Desert Storm. He enrolled in college and — true to the discipline he carried home from the service — did not sit still. He started a small part-time business called Tubs of Fun! out of a simple drive to build something of his own.

What started as a part-time venture became a full-time passion. Troy built Derheim Inc into the most recognized pool and spa company in the Fargo-Moorhead area — earning seven consecutive APSP Region 5 Awards of Excellence and induction into the Aqua National Pool Dealers Hall of Fame in 2009. He builds backyard pools, indoor aquatic facilities, and commercial spa installations. The scale changes. The standard does not.

"I started with the idea that if you take care of people — really take care of them — the business takes care of itself. Twenty-eight years later, I still believe that."

Troy is personally involved in every project. He will walk your property, listen to what you want, and come back with ideas you probably have not thought of yet. That is not marketing language — it is just how he works.
