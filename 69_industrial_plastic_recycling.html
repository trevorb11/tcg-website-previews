Who We Are

USA's Reliable
Plastic Recyclers

Industrial Plastic Recycling Inc (IPR Corp) is a privately-held corporation headquartered in South Holland, Illinois, dedicated to one thing: taking industrial plastic waste and turning it back into valuable raw material.

We serve manufacturers across the United States — buying their scrap plastic streams, processing the material through our industrial facility, and selling high-quality recycled pellets and regrind to injection molders, extruders, thermoformers, and compounders who need reliable, consistent feedstock.

We built IPR on the belief that responsible recycling and a profitable business aren't in conflict — they're the same thing. Every pound of plastic we process is one less pound in a landfill, and one more pound putting your supply chain to work.

"We strive to minimize the carbon footprint on a global level to make the environment more sustainable and protect nature from getting overburdened by excessive plastic usage."

What We Do

Full-Service Plastic
Processing

From the moment your scrap material leaves your facility to the point where recycled pellets reach the next manufacturer — we manage the full cycle.

📊

Collection & Acquisition

We buy industrial scrap, trim, off-spec material, and post-industrial plastic in bulk. Competitive pricing, reliable scheduling, and straightforward transactions — we make selling your scrap simple.

🔢

Shredding & Grinding

Industrial-scale size reduction of plastic materials to specification for downstream processing. Consistent particle size output for downstream pelletizing or direct regrind use.

⚙

Plastic Pelletizing

Processing recycled plastic into reusable pellets that meet the quality specifications of injection molders, extruders, and other plastic processors. Consistent output for consistent production.

📋

Sorting & Categorizing

Identification and separation of plastics by resin type, color, and chemical characteristics. Proper categorization is the foundation of high-quality recycled material — we do it right.

📦

Baling & Palletizing

Preparation of processed material for efficient storage, transport, and delivery. Baled and palletized to industry standards for seamless integration into your receiving process.

🛀

Material Supply

Selling high-quality recycled plastic material to manufacturers who need consistent, reliable feedstock. We supply processors with the recycled material their operations depend on.

Environmental Commitment

Every Pound Matters

We started IPR Corp because we believe industrial plastic recycling is one of the most straightforward ways to meaningfully reduce environmental waste — and it just happens to make good business sense. There's no conflict between doing the right thing and running a profitable operation. They reinforce each other.

Every pound of plastic we process is a pound that doesn't end up in a landfill, incinerator, or the environment. It's also a pound of virgin plastic that doesn't need to be produced — saving energy, raw materials, and the carbon that comes with them.

🌿

Diverting plastic waste from landfills and keeping it in productive use within the manufacturing supply chain

⚡

Reducing the energy consumption and carbon emissions associated with producing virgin plastic from raw materials

🔄

Closing the loop — turning one manufacturer's waste into another's raw material, over and over again
