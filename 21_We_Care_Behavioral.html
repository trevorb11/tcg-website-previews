Who We Are

Real People. Real Care.
Right Here in Montana.

We Care Behavioral Health grew from a belief that is still at the center of everything we do: asking for help takes courage, and the people who do deserve to be met with warmth and without judgment.

Allexendra St. Clair is a licensed clinical social worker who has dedicated her career to serving Montana communities. She and our team don't see clients as cases -- they see people. Seeking help is not a sign of weakness. It is one of the most honest and brave things a person can do.

Our Team

Compassionate Professionals Who Genuinely Care

Our team is made up of people who chose this work because they believe in it. Every member brings clinical expertise, lived empathy, and a commitment to walking beside you.

Clinical Therapy Team
