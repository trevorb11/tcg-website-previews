About IAP GSG

Protecting Your Investment. Managing Your Project.

Founded in 2004, IAP Government Services Group was built around a simple idea: government and institutional owners deserve the same project management sophistication that large private developers get as a matter of course. Public entities should not have to navigate complex construction projects alone.

IAP serves as your Owner's Representative — a professional advocate who manages the entire project on your behalf. We handle pre-construction planning, competitive bidding, contractor oversight, and final closeout so you can stay focused on your mission while we protect your budget and timeline.

Based in Columbus, Ohio, we have managed capital improvement projects for government bodies, school districts, municipal agencies, and institutional clients across the region and beyond. Every project benefits from our full attention and a management process built around your priorities.

What We Do

Full-Service Project Management

IAP offers four core service lines covering every phase of a capital improvement project — from the first planning meeting to final punch list.

Pre-Construction & GC Services

We manage the pre-construction phase end to end — scoping, estimating, scheduling, and contractor selection. Our competitive bidding process ensures you receive the best value while meeting all applicable procurement requirements.

Capital Improvements Program

Multi-year capital planning and execution management for agencies with ongoing facility needs. We help prioritize projects, develop realistic budgets, and coordinate execution across multiple simultaneous improvements.

Owner's Project Representation

IAP stands in as your representative in all contractor and designer communications. We keep your interests first, enforce contract terms, and make sure every decision serves your project goals — not the contractor's.

Turn-Key Construction Management

For clients who want complete hands-off delivery, IAP manages the full construction process — daily oversight, RFI management, change order review, payment application review, and project closeout documentation.

Our Method

Our Project Process

Every IAP project follows a disciplined four-phase process designed to deliver on time and within budget.

01

Assessment & Planning

We assess your facility needs, establish scope, develop a realistic budget, and build a project schedule that works for your organization

02

Public Bidding & Procurement

We publicly advertise using your preferred method plus IAP platforms to ensure competitive bids and full procurement compliance

03

Construction Management

We manage the contractor, track progress against the schedule, handle RFIs and change orders, and keep you informed at every step

04

Completion & Closeout

We conduct final inspections, assemble closeout documentation, and ensure all punch list items are resolved before final payment is released
