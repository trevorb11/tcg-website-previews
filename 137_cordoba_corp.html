Our Story

A Legacy of Excellence in Public Service

Cordoba Corporation was founded in 1983 by George L. Pla with a clear mission: to build a firm that combines technical excellence with an authentic commitment to the communities it serves. Over four decades, that commitment has shaped every project we touch and every team we build.

Headquartered in Los Angeles, we have grown to nearly 600 professionals working across nine California offices. We serve the full spectrum of public infrastructure — from high-speed rail corridors and metro transit systems to water reclamation facilities and public school campuses. No matter the sector, our approach remains the same: listen deeply, plan rigorously, and deliver.

As a certified Disadvantaged and Minority Business Enterprise, Cordoba stands as proof that excellence and community representation are not competing ideals. We are proud to be a model for diversity in the engineering and construction management professions.

"We built this company on the belief that great infrastructure starts with people — the people who build it and the people who depend on it every day."

George L. Pla, Founder & Chief Executive Officer

What We Do

End-to-End Program Delivery

From the earliest planning stages through construction completion and closeout, Cordoba provides the full range of management services that complex public infrastructure demands.

01
Program Management

We serve as the owner's trusted representative for large-scale, multi-project capital programs — providing strategic oversight, budget control, schedule governance, and stakeholder coordination from inception through delivery.

  • Capital program planning and governance
  • Owner's Representative services
  • Multi-project portfolio oversight
  • Long-range cost and schedule modeling
  • Grant funding management and reporting
02
Project Management

At the individual project level, our project managers bring discipline and clarity to delivery — managing scope, budget, schedule, and risk with precision while keeping all stakeholders informed and aligned.

  • Scope definition and control
  • Schedule development and monitoring
  • Budget management and change control
  • Risk identification and mitigation
  • Third-party coordination and permitting
03
Construction Management

Our construction managers operate at the field level, maintaining safety standards, quality controls, and schedule adherence. We are the bridge between design intent and built reality, protecting the owner's interests every step of the way.

  • Resident engineering and inspection
  • Quality assurance and quality control
  • Safety compliance oversight
  • Contractor relations and RFI management
  • Claims avoidance and dispute resolution
04
Engineering Support

Our engineering professionals provide analytical and technical support across the project lifecycle, from feasibility assessments and preliminary design reviews to constructability analysis and systems integration.

  • Feasibility and alternatives analysis
  • Design review and value engineering
  • Constructability assessments
  • Systems integration support
  • Technical studies and reports

Projects That Define California

Transportation
California High-Speed Rail

Cordoba serves as a key program management contractor on the California High-Speed Rail Authority's Central Valley segment — the largest public infrastructure investment in the state's history. Our team supports environmental review, design oversight, right-of-way coordination, and construction management across hundreds of miles of alignment.

Client CA HSR Authority
Role Program Management
Transportation
LA Metro Eastside Extension

The Metro Gold Line Eastside Extension brought light rail service to East Los Angeles for the first time, connecting communities long underserved by regional transit. Cordoba provided program and project management support across design, construction, and systems integration phases of this landmark project.

Client LA Metro
Role Project Management

The City of Los Angeles Bureau of Sanitation's Safe Clean Water Program represents a multi-billion dollar investment in sustainable water infrastructure.
