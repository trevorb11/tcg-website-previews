Our Story

Built from the Ground Up by People Who Know the Work

Auvidatel was founded in 2003 by brothers Daniel, Mario, and Michael Insua — a family who believed that quality installation labor could be delivered at a level the industry rarely saw. What started as a small audio, video, data, and telephone subcontractor in Winter Haven, Florida has grown into one of the state's most trusted low-voltage installation firms.

Recognizing an unmet need in the security and life safety space, they expanded their services and never looked back. Today, Auvidatel operates four offices across Florida and delivers turnkey installation services for some of the nation's largest companies.

  • Trusted by Fortune 500 companies as an installation labor partner
  • Four Florida offices: Winter Haven, Stuart, Longwood, and Tampa
  • Deep expertise in security, life safety, and access control systems
  • Led by founders who still know every aspect of the work firsthand

Commercial Environments

Security Systems

Life Safety Systems

Access Control

How We Work

Four Steps from Scope to Handoff

Our process is built to make your job easier. No surprises, no delays from our end, and full documentation at every stage.

01

Project Assessment
