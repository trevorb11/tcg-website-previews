What We Offer

Comprehensive Care for Every Aspect of Your Health

HealthSource offers more than spinal adjustments. Our progressive care model integrates multiple treatment approaches to create lasting improvement in how you feel and function.

Chiropractic Adjustments

Precise, gentle spinal manipulation to restore proper alignment, relieve pressure on nerves, and reduce pain at its source. Our technique is always adapted to your comfort and clinical needs.

Functional Rehabilitation

Guided therapeutic exercises designed to restore strength, flexibility, and movement patterns. These programs address the underlying weakness and imbalances that allow injuries to recur.

Spinal Decompression

Non-surgical traction therapy that gently stretches the spine, relieving pressure on compressed discs and nerves. Particularly effective for herniated discs and chronic lower back pain.

Laser Therapy

Low-level laser therapy that penetrates deep into tissue, stimulating cellular repair and reducing inflammation without pain or recovery time. An excellent complement to manual treatment.

Nutritional Support

Inflammation is often rooted in diet and lifestyle. We provide practical nutritional guidance and targeted supplement recommendations that support healing from the inside.

Sleep Optimization

Poor sleep drives chronic pain, and chronic pain disrupts sleep. We address the sleep quality issues that undermine recovery and help our patients break the cycle for good.

Ongoing Wellness

The Wellness Club: Don't Wait for Pain

Our subscription-based Wellness Club is designed for patients who understand that maintaining your health is better -- and less expensive -- than recovering from pain. Prescheduled maintenance visits keep your body functioning at its best so issues never escalate into injuries.

Automatic Scheduling

Maintenance visits are pre-scheduled at intervals determined by your provider -- no remembering to call, no gaps in care.

Predictable Cost

Prevention Over Recovery

Members who maintain consistent care experience fewer acute episodes, faster recovery from minor strain, and greater overall quality of life.
