How We Help

Neuropsychological Services

We offer a full range of evaluation and rehabilitation services, delivered with the care, thoroughness, and transparency that meaningful progress requires.

Neuropsychological Evaluation

Comprehensive assessment of cognitive functioning, memory, attention, and executive skills to clarify diagnosis and guide treatment.

Clinical Consultation

Individualized consultation and treatment planning for individuals, families, and referring providers navigating neurological and psychological challenges.

Cognitive Rehabilitation

Evidence-based rehabilitation programs designed to rebuild cognitive skills and help individuals regain independence following injury or illness.

Reintegration Support

Practical support for individuals transitioning back into the community following incarceration, hospitalization, or extended treatment — because the path forward matters.

Forensic Neuropsychology

Expert evaluation and testimony services for legal matters involving neuropsychological issues, including criminal cases, civil litigation, and disability assessments.

Who We Serve

Everyone Deserves the Right Support

Project ReEntry was built to serve individuals and families at pivotal moments — when the path forward requires expertise, empathy, and a clear plan.

Brain Injury Recovery

About Our Practice

Dr. Richard Pollock, PhD

Dr. Richard Pollock is a licensed Clinical Neuropsychologist practicing in Houston, Texas, with extensive experience in neuropsychological assessment, cognitive rehabilitation, and forensic neuropsychology. He founded Project ReEntry with a simple conviction: that every person, regardless of their history or circumstances, deserves access to expert psychological care and the opportunity to build a better future.

His work spans a broad range of clinical and applied settings — from evaluating individuals with traumatic brain injuries to supporting those making the difficult transition back into community life following incarceration. Dr. Pollock brings both the scientific rigor of a trained neuropsychologist and the warmth of a clinician who genuinely cares about outcomes.
