Our Programs

Care and Learning at Every Stage

We offer a continuum of programs designed to meet children exactly where they are developmentally — from the earliest weeks of life through the start of middle school.

Ages 6 weeks – 12 months

Infant Care

Warm, attentive care in a safe, stimulating environment. Our infant teachers build genuine relationships with each baby and communicate with parents throughout every day.

Ages 12 – 30 months

Toddlers

Structured play and early language development for the busy toddler years. Our classrooms are designed to encourage exploration, movement, and the joy of early discovery.

Ages 2.5 – 3 years

Early Preschool

Building social skills, pre-reading foundations, and curiosity in a classroom designed around child-led learning and teacher-guided activities that prepare children for preschool.

Ages 3 – 4 years

Preschool

Creative, inquiry-based learning that introduces early literacy, numeracy, science, and social studies concepts through hands-on activities, storytelling, and collaborative play.

Ages 4 – 5 years

Pre-Kindergarten

A rigorous but joyful curriculum designed to close the kindergarten readiness gap. Children who complete our Pre-K program arrive on the first day of school confident and ready to learn.

Ages 5 – 12 years

Before & After School + Summer Camp

Safe, enriching before and after school care plus summer camp for school-age children. Homework help, enrichment activities, outdoor play, and friendships that last beyond the school year.

Our Approach

Learning Through Doing. Growing Through Play.

We believe children learn best when they are engaged, curious, and having fun. Our curriculum is built around developmentally appropriate, play-based learning that builds the academic, social, and emotional skills children need to succeed.

Every age group has a carefully designed curriculum with daily structure that balances teacher-led instruction with child-initiated exploration. Our teachers are trained not just to manage classrooms but to observe children thoughtfully and respond to what each child individually needs to grow.

School readiness is not just about knowing letters and numbers. It is about being able to sit and listen, ask questions, share and take turns, manage frustration, and try again after getting something wrong. We build all of it, every day.

Safety & Peace of Mind

We Take the Trust You Place in Us Seriously

Leaving your child in someone else's care takes trust. We have spent over fifty years working to deserve it. Our Norman center operates under strict safety protocols, with controlled access entry, daily health checks, and trained staff who know how to handle emergencies with calm and competence.

Every teacher at La Petite Academy undergoes thorough background screening before their first day and receives ongoing training in early childhood education, first aid, and child safety throughout their time with us.

And because we know parents need to feel connected even when they cannot be there, our SproutAbout app gives you a live view of your child's classroom, photo updates throughout the day, and a direct line to your child's teacher.
