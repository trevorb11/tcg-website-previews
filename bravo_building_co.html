Leadership

Gregory Di Loreto

Founder & CEO

Greg Di Loreto founded Bravo Building Co. in 1982 with a straightforward philosophy: build with integrity, deliver on your promises, and treat every project as if your own name were on the building. Over four decades later, that philosophy remains the foundation of everything Bravo does.

Under Greg's leadership, Bravo has grown from a local California operation into a multi-state general contracting firm serving clients from Arizona to Montana. His hands-on management style and deep construction expertise have earned Bravo a BuildZoom score of 126, placing the firm in the top 1% of all California licensed contractors.

"Our reputation was built one project at a time. After forty years, there are no shortcuts to earning trust, and we have never looked for any."

Greg remains actively involved in project oversight and client relationships, ensuring the same personal accountability that built Bravo continues in every project the company undertakes. Beyond business, he is a committed supporter of the Wounded Warrior Project and Freedom Alliance.
