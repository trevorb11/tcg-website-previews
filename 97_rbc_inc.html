About RBC Inc.

Five Decades of Federal Excellence

RBC, Incorporated was founded in 1975 with a clear mission: deliver innovative solutions for the acquisition, development, and support of complex computer-based systems for the federal government. We have spent five decades earning and keeping that trust.

We are recognized as an industry leader in information and engineering management. Our teams understand the full lifecycle of government programs — from acquisition strategy through system sustainment — and we bring that depth to every engagement we take on.

Our primary offices in Alexandria, Virginia and Lexington Park, Maryland position us precisely where our clients need us: close to the agencies we support and minutes from Patuxent River Naval Air Station, one of the country's largest defense aviation facilities.

What We Do

Core Capabilities

RBC brings deep technical expertise across the full spectrum of government information and engineering management — from acquisition to sustainment.

Systems Acquisition Support

We guide federal clients through the complexities of defense and civilian acquisition — from program planning and requirements definition through contract award and program execution. Our team understands acquisition policy and translates it into workable program strategy.

Engineering Management

Managing complex, multi-discipline engineering programs requires both technical depth and program management discipline. We bring both, with teams experienced in systems engineering, integration, test, and evaluation for some of the government's most demanding programs.

Information Systems Development

Our TechnoLogic division specializes in the development and support of advanced information systems for government clients. We build systems that work in the demanding, security-conscious environments federal agencies require — reliable, compliant, and maintainable.

Technical Program Support

RBC provides embedded technical support to government program offices, bringing expert personnel to supplement government teams on complex programs. We have supported some of the nation's most sensitive and consequential programs for more than forty years.

Security. Compliance. Accountability.

Federal clients deserve a contractor who treats security as a foundation, not a checkbox. RBC has maintained the personnel, facility, and program security infrastructure necessary to support classified programs throughout our history. We meet our clients where they are — and where they need us to be.
