# BC United Contractors -- Comprehensive Website Plan

## 1. Market Context

### Competitive Landscape: Jefferson Parish / West Bank / Greater New Orleans

The general contracting market on the west bank of Jefferson Parish is fragmented, with a mix of well-established multi-generational firms, mid-tier operators, and small owner-operated outfits -- many of the latter having weak or nonexistent web presences. This creates a real opening for a new entrant who shows up online looking polished and professional from day one.

**Competitor Analysis (5 local firms reviewed):**

| Competitor | Website | Positioning | Strengths | Weaknesses |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Quillen Construction Group** | quillenconstructiongroup.com | Premium residential + commercial | Gold-and-black branding, Elementor-built, 20+ years, Google 5-star, mobile project tracker | Sets a high bar |
| **Continental Construction** | continentalconstruction.org | Residential remodeler | Blue-and-white corporate look, extensive blog/SEO, license numbers, location-based landing pages | Agency-built (Argent Marketing) |
| **Lauricella & Sons** | remodelerneworleans.com | Historic home restoration + foundation repair | 3 generations, BBB A+, 5-star reviews, 10+ service sub-pages | Niche focus (historic/structural) |
| **BC Construction & Design** | bccnola.com | Premium new construction | Minimalist photography-driven design, 18 years, state-licensed, family-owned | Very small site (4 pages), no SEO |
| **Flores Gamez Construction** | fgconstructionllc.company | Budget-friendly remodeling + handyman | 12+ years, bilingual, WhatsApp contact | Essentially a "coming soon" placeholder |

**Key Takeaways:**
- The top competitors have agency-built sites with SEO, blogs, and location pages. They are the benchmark.
- Several competitors have NO functional website at all. BC United can immediately leapfrog these.
- Trust signals are critical: license numbers, insurance, years in business, reviews, BBB rating.
- Location-specific landing pages (Avondale, Gretna, Harvey, Marrero, Westwego) are a proven SEO tactic.
- The west bank is underserved compared to Metairie/Kenner -- fewer contractors specifically claim it as home turf.

**Market Conditions:**
- Avondale population: ~5,257, median home value ~$130,400, 85% owner-occupied housing, median housing age 1970 (significant renovation demand)
- Jefferson Parish median home prices rose ~6% in 2025; Westwego posted double-digit gains
- Housing stock from the 1960s-1970s = high demand for kitchen/bath remodels, roof work, and general updates

**The Opportunity:**
BC United's home base in Avondale puts it squarely on the west bank, an area where most competitors are headquartered in Metairie, Harahan, or New Orleans proper. By branding as THE west bank contractor, BC United can own a geographic niche.

## 6. Content Strategy

### Tone of Voice
- Professional but approachable, not corporate, not overly casual
- First person plural ("we" / "our team")
- Emphasize reliability, quality, and local roots
- Speak directly to homeowners' concerns: budget, timeline, trust, quality

### Building Credibility from Zero Reviews
1. Jackoby's personal statement on homepage and About page
2. License and insurance numbers displayed prominently
3. "Our Promise" or guarantee statement
4. Before/after project photos as visual proof
5. Request reviews aggressively from first 5-10 customers
6. Respond to every review publicly
7. Video testimonials from early customers
8. Community involvement mentions

## 9. Technical Recommendations

### Platform: WordPress + Elementor
- WordPress powers 43% of the web; massive ecosystem
- Elementor Pro ($59/year) for visual editing
- Alternative: GeneratePress + GenerateBlocks for lighter/faster approach

### Hosting
- SiteGround ($3-14/month) or Cloudways ($14/month)
- Avoid GoDaddy, Bluehost, EIG brands

### Essential Plugins
- Rank Math SEO or Yoast SEO
- WPForms Lite or Gravity Forms
- Smush or ShortPixel (image compression)
- WP Rocket or LiteSpeed Cache
- UpdraftPlus (backups)
- Wordfence (security)
- Google Site Kit
- MonsterInsights or GA4 direct

### Analytics
- Google Analytics 4
- Google Search Console
- Google Tag Manager
- Microsoft Clarity (free heatmaps)
