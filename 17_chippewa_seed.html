The Growing Season

With You Every Step

From spring soil prep to fall harvest, Chippewa Farm Supply is your partner at every stage of the northeast Michigan growing season.

April – May

Plant

Seed selection, soil testing, and planting. Get your corn, soybeans, and alfalfa in the ground with the right varieties for your fields.

May – June

Fertilize

Apply starter fertilizer, sidedress nitrogen, and build your nutrient program. Custom blends matched to your soil analysis.

June – August

Protect

Herbicide applications, fungicide timing, and scouting. Keep your crop clean and protected through the critical growing months.

September – October

Harvest

Bring it home. Then plan ahead with cover crop seeding and fall fertilizer to set up next year’s success.

Local Expertise

Curated for
Alcona County

Northeast Michigan presents unique growing conditions that set it apart from the rest of the state. Shorter growing seasons, sandy-loam soils, and variable spring weather mean that seed selection and input timing are critical. What works in central Michigan does not always perform up here.

That is why Chippewa Farm Supply carefully curates our seed lineup and fertilizer recommendations specifically for Alcona County and the surrounding region. We know this ground, we know this climate, and we stock the products that have proven themselves in these fields season after season.
