Our Story

Texas-Built, Texas-Proud

Double S Services has been a trusted name in portable buildings and structures across Central Texas for over 18 years. Located at the crossroads of Highway 7 and Highway 79 near Lake Limestone in Marquez, TX, we serve farmers, ranchers, homeowners, and businesses throughout the region.

Owner Sheri Schmal built this business on a straightforward promise: give customers a quality product at a fair price, stand behind the work, and make the whole process easy. No runaround, no hidden fees — just solid structures and honest service.

Whether you need a simple storage shed for tools, a metal carport to protect your vehicles, a finished cabin for a weekend retreat, or you need an existing building relocated, we have the experience and the equipment to get it done right.

  • Proudly based in Marquez, TX — Hwy 7 & Hwy 79
  • Serving Leon County and surrounding Central Texas counties
  • Full delivery and professional setup on every order
  • Custom sizing and finishing options available on most structures
  • Rent-to-own financing available — no credit check required

How It Works

From Quote to Move-In Ready

Getting your new building is simple. Here's what to expect from start to finish.

01

Choose Your Building

Browse our selection in person at our Marquez lot or call us to discuss your needs. We'll help you find the right size and style for your property and budget.

02

Get Your Quote

We provide clear, upfront pricing with no hidden fees. If you want customizations, we'll spec it out and give you an exact number before anything moves forward.

03

Prepare Your Site

We'll walk you through simple site prep requirements — typically just a level, accessible area. We'll let you know exactly what to have ready before delivery day.

04

Delivery & Setup

Our crew delivers and professionally sets up your building at the agreed location. We make sure everything is level, secure, and looking great before we leave.

05

Enjoy It

Your new building is ready to use from day one. If you ever have questions or need anything adjusted, we're just a phone call away.
