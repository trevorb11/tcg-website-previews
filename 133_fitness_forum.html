We've built a facility with more amenities, more programming, and more community than you'd expect -- because we believe the environment you train in should be the best part of the experience, not an obstacle to it.

The Facility

Everything Under One Roof

Built for people who take their health seriously -- whatever that looks like for them. We've invested in the equipment, the space, and the amenities so you never have to compromise.

Cardio Floor

Treadmills, ellipticals, stationary bikes, rowing machines, and stair climbers -- all current-generation equipment with digital displays and performance tracking.

Strength & Free Weights

Dumbbells, barbells, squat racks, benches, cable machines, and a full complement of plate-loaded and selectorized strength equipment for every training style.

Group Fitness Studio

A dedicated studio with sprung flooring, sound system, and mirrors -- designed for everything from yoga and Pilates to high-intensity cycling and boot camp classes.

Aquatics Pool

A full-length lap pool and therapy pool, with a hot tub for recovery and relaxation. Aquatic classes, swim lessons, and open lane swimming for all members.

Indoor Track

An elevated indoor track above the main gym floor -- ideal for warm-ups, walking, running, and anything that benefits from a consistent surface year-round.

Courts, Sauna & More

Racquetball and pickleball courts, electric dry sauna, steam room, tanning, and child care -- because taking care of yourself shouldn't require leaving your life behind.

Specialty Programs

Built for Specific Needs

Beyond the main floor and group classes, Fitness Forum offers specialty programs designed for members with specific health conditions, goals, or interests.

Seniors 60+

Low-impact strength, mobility, and balance classes designed specifically for active aging and long-term health.

Rock Steady Boxing

A non-contact boxing program proven to benefit people with Parkinson's disease and related movement disorders.

Martial Arts

Structured martial arts classes for youth and adults covering fundamentals, self-defense, and discipline.

Youth Programs
